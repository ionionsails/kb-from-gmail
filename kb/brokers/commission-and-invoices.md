# Broker commission and commission invoices

Commission is shown in the broker offer as a percentage taken after the discount. It is fixed. The broker invoices it to the boat ownership company, not to us and not to the guest.

The offer shows the price after discounts, the commission and the net total to us. Boat prices depend on dates and availability and live only in the offers, see [Prices](../policies/prices.md) and [Discounts](../policies/discounts.md). How the commission is calculated: [D-012](../decisions/open.md#d-012).

## How we answer

Broker asks for a higher commission or a bonus:

> Unfortunately the commission cannot be modified, as we do not have a policy that allows such changes. We look at the quality of our cooperation: timely payments, payments made directly by the client into our account, accurate commission invoices and complete documents before the client arrives.

Broker asks for an extra discount:

> You may offer an additional discount of up to 5% from your commission.

Early check-in or free extras requested:

> Early embarkation is provided with external partners, including the cleaning teams who prepare the yacht in advance, so we cannot offer it free of charge. We can offer the outboard engine as a gesture of goodwill for this booking.

Commission invoice request after payment:

> Thank you, we have received the payment. May I also have your commission invoice for this booking. Please issue it to the boat ownership company shown on the charter party. We need it before embarkation and within the same month it is issued.

Invoice missing:

> I am missing your commission invoice for this booking. The details are: [details]. The amount is [amount].

Wrong invoice details:

> The invoice shows the wrong company details. Could you please reissue it to the boat ownership company below.

New agency asking for 20%:

> Before I prepare the offer, can you issue a commission invoice, and are you a travel agency or a broker? The commission has to be invoiced. If you cannot invoice, I can offer a NET package instead.
