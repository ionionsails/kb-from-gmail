# Your Option on our [Boat]

The email we send when an option is placed, mostly to a broker, or as one paragraph inside a reply to a direct client.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | `IonionSails: Your Option on our [Boat]!` | brokers | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r4969412333591998314) |
| When we send it | Right after we place an option for a broker or a client. | brokers, direct clients | all | see examples |
| Greeting | Dear Partners, (or the broker contact's first name) | brokers | all | see examples |
| Direct clients | No separate email. One paragraph in the reply with the offer. | direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r2016699508300997895) |
| Option length | Lives on [Options](../policies/options.md). Use [option end date] in the text. | all | all | see [D-001](../decisions/open.md#d-001) |
| Attachment | One yacht block with the standard footer. No charter price in the body in the versions seen. | brokers | all | see examples |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team (or the sender's name and Chartering Department) | all | all | see examples |

## How we answer

Broker version.

Subject: IonionSails: Your Option on our [Boat]!

Dear Partners,

Thank you for your option on our [Boat] for the week commencing on [Date]. The option is valid until [option end date].

You can find the details of the yacht below.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Best regards,
IonionSails Team

[Yacht block and standard footer]

Direct client version, one paragraph inside the reply with the offer.

I have placed an option on [Boat] for [Dates] in your name. It is not binding. It gives you priority for the yacht until [option end date], so you do not lose the offer. If you decide before that date, let me know and I will send you the booking documents.

## Phrases cleaned

| Real email | In the approved text |
| --- | --- |
| I hope this email finds you well. | Removed. The email starts with the thanks. |
| Should you need any further information or assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us. | If you have any questions, just reply to this email. |
| If there is an update prior to the expiration date feel free to let us know. | If you decide before that date, let me know. |
| Essentially, this is a pre-booking process to secure your preferred yacht. | It gives you priority for the yacht until [option end date]. |
