# Deposit explanation (Option A and Option B)

Our reply when a client asks how the security deposit works. We explain the two options and do not quote amounts in the template.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | Reply in the booking thread, `Re: IonionSails: Welcome on Board ([booking ref]) - [SURNAME]`, or the client's own subject | direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r6687077659634441532) |
| When we send it | The client asks about the deposit after receiving the documents or the offer. | direct clients, charterers of brokers | all | see examples |
| Option A | Independent deposit insurance (Yacht-Pool, Hamburg, Fairwinds or another company), arranged before arrival. The refundable deposit is pre-authorised on the card at check-in and released after the return. | all | all | see examples |
| Option B | Damage Waiver on arrival, through Yacht-Pool. A non-refundable fee plus a refundable part, which the client can insure on the spot. | all | all | see examples |
| Amounts | Not in the template. They live on [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md). Use [amount] and link or attach the booking confirmation. | per boat | all | not found – please confirm |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team | all | all | see examples |

## How we answer

Subject: Re: [client's subject]

Dear Mr./Mrs. [first name],

Thank you for your email. We offer two ways to cover the security deposit.

Option A. You take independent deposit insurance with Yacht-Pool, Hamburg, Fairwinds or another company. You arrange it before arrival. At check-in we pre-authorise the refundable deposit of [amount] on your card, and we release it after you return the yacht.

Option B. You choose the Damage Waiver through Yacht-Pool when you arrive. You pay a non-refundable fee of [amount] and a refundable deposit of [amount]. You can insure the refundable part on the spot if you wish.

Both options are shown on your booking confirmation. Tell me which one you prefer and I will update the documents.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Follow-up when the client has chosen Option B: Yes, you have understood correctly. With Option B no additional security deposit is required or pre-authorised. I am sending you the updated documents with the correct amounts. (Check the amounts on [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md) first. Rules for Option B with a skipper are still open, see D-018.)
