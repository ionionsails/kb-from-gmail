# Follow-up on an unanswered offer

Three automatic emails after an offer with no reply, plus a manual follow-up and a reply when the client declines.

## How we answer

Email 1. Subject: IonionSails: Following our offer!

Dear [first name],

I am following up on our offer for [Boat] and [Dates]. Did it reach you?

If you have special requests, tell me and I will adjust the offer.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Email 2. Subject: IonionSails: Do not lose the offer!

Dear [first name],

Emails sometimes end up in spam. If you still plan a sailing holiday, reply here and I will check availability again.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Email 3. Subject: IonionSails: Future bookings!

Dear [first name],

We have not heard from you, so we assume your plans have changed. If you book with us directly in the future, you get a free SUP.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Manual follow-up in the thread.

Do you have any news on the offer we sent you? If you need anything else, just reply to this email.

When the client declines.

Thank you for letting us know. We are sorry you cannot join us this time. We hope to arrange your sailing holiday another time. Stay in touch, and we hope to see you in Greece.
