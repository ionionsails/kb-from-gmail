# Welcome on Board (booking documents)

The email that sends the booking documents to the charterer. Often preceded by a short thank-you for the booking.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | `IonionSails: Welcome on Board ([booking ref]) - [SURNAME] !` | direct clients, charterers of brokers | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r6687077659634441532) |
| When we send it | After the booking is accepted and we have the charterer details. When exactly a booking counts as confirmed: see D-007. | all | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r1355434502876477843) |
| Step before | Short reply: thank you for the booking, documents follow in a separate email. Returning guests: we are glad to have them back. | brokers, direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1865690862825955509) |
| Documents | Booking Confirmation (payment terms, bank details), Charter Party (signed copy back), Boarding Pass with yacht details and crew list link. Other packages exist, see D-007. | all | all | see examples |
| Payment reference | Booking confirmation number | all | all | see [D-010](../decisions/open.md#d-010) |
| Crew list | Deadline lives on [Booking documents](../policies/booking-documents.md). Placeholder: [crew list deadline]. | all | all | see [D-008](../decisions/open.md#d-008) |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team (the real emails come from the Charter Manager, the Chartering Department or Thalia, CEO) | all | all | see examples |

## How we answer

Subject: IonionSails: Welcome on Board ([booking ref]) - [SURNAME]

Dear Mr./Mrs. [first name],

Thank you for choosing IonionSails. Your booking documents are attached.

1. Booking Confirmation. It has the payment terms and our bank details. Please use the booking confirmation number as the payment reference when you pay.
2. Charter Party. Please sign it and send a copy back to us.
3. Boarding Pass. It has the details of your yacht and a link to your crew list. Please fill in the crew list at least [crew list deadline] before your arrival.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

We look forward to having you with us in Lefkada.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Note: the numbered list is kept here because it is a document list inside the email. Remove it if the sender prefers plain sentences. After the booking is paid, the closing can be "See you on board,".
