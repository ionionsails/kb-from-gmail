# Regarding Your Inquiry (first reply to the website form)

Our first reply to a direct client who wrote through the website. It has three versions: short question, offer, and yacht not available.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | `IonionSails: Regarding Your Inquiry!` | direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r4066769281993591478) |
| When we send it | Within the day, when the inquiry has no dates or no skipper answer (short version), or enough details for an offer (offer version). | direct clients | all | see examples |

| Yacht not available | Explain the problem, apologise, and send alternatives. | booking made but yacht lost | all | see examples |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team | all | all | see examples |

## How we answer

Short version.

Subject: IonionSails: Regarding Your Inquiry!

Dear Mr./Mrs. [first name],

Thank you for contacting IonionSails. We would be glad to have you on board.

Which dates are you interested in, and do you need a skipper? Once I know, I will check availability and send you an offer.

Send me a message on WhatsApp if it's easier for you.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

Offer version, opening only. Continue with the text and blocks from [Our offer](our-offer.md).

Thank you for contacting IonionSails. Based on your inquiry, I have prepared an offer with yachts that fit your charter in [Month, Year]. Please find it below.

Yacht not available version.

I am writing about your reservation. One of our yachts is no longer available for your dates because of an operational problem. Your booking has not been secured with a payment yet, so I wanted to tell you right away. I am sorry for the inconvenience. I have checked the best alternatives for your dates and you can find them below.

Let me know which one you prefer and I will hold it for you.

## Phrases cleaned

| May I ask which dates you are interested in ... | Which dates are you interested in, and do you need a skipper? |

| While this is a very unusual circumstance, we apologize ... and appreciate your understanding. | I am sorry for the inconvenience. |
| Please feel free to share any preferences or feedback | Removed. The sender asks for dates directly. |
