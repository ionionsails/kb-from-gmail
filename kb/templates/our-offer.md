# Our Offer (direct client offer)

Our first offer to a direct client who asked for a yacht or dates. A short personal text on top, then one block per yacht from the booking system.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | `IonionSails: Our Offer!` (as a reply, the client's own subject may stay) | direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r9082496080500929761) |
| Variant with 2027 advisor intro | `IonionSails: Your 2027 Sailing Holidays!` (day cruise: `Your 2026 Daily Cruise!`) | direct clients, personal advisor style | 2027 | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r4317796617365042088) |
| When we send it | After the first inquiry, once we know dates, group size and skipper or bareboat. If dates are missing, send [Regarding your inquiry](regarding-your-inquiry.md) first. | direct clients | all | see examples |
| Greeting | Dear Mr./Mrs. [first name], | direct clients | all | see examples |
| Sign-off | Best regards, then the sender's name and Chartering Department. Default signature: IonionSails Team. | all | all | see examples |
| Yacht blocks | Pasted from the booking system: period (Sat 17:00 to Sat 09:00), type, year, base, length, berths, cabins, list price, discount lines, final price, extras, equipment. Prices and discounts are not repeated here, see [Discounts](../policies/discounts.md). | all yachts | all | see examples |
| Footer | Standard footer under the yacht blocks (return time, what the price includes, deposit Options A and B, offer validity, VAT paragraph). Full Greek VAT wording: not found – please confirm. | all | all | see examples |

Structure of the email text:

1. Thanks for the interest.
2. Who writes, and that we help through the booking.
3. One paragraph on the client's own request (boat size, availability, alternative, skipper or hostess).
4. Weekly charter, Saturday to Saturday.
5. Links to the Ionian Sailing Guide and the suggested itineraries.
6. Offer and Free Cancellation terms are valid for a limited time.
7. Closing line.
8. Yacht blocks and footer.

## How we answer

Subject: IonionSails: Our Offer!

Dear [Mr./Mrs. first name],

Thank you for contacting IonionSails.

My name is [first name] and I am part of our Chartering Department. I can help you with the booking from the first offer to your arrival in Lefkada.

[One or two sentences on the request. Example: Our [Boat] is available for [Dates]. It has [cabins] cabins, so it suits your group of [number] people.]

We charter weekly, from Saturday to Saturday. Our yachts are privately owned and maintained all year round. The offer is below, with the details of each yacht.

You can find our Ionian Sailing Guide here: [link]. Our suggested itineraries are here: [link].

The offer and our Free Cancellation terms are available for a limited time. If you would like us to hold a yacht for you, tell me and I will place an option.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email. Send me a message on WhatsApp if it's easier for you.

Kind regards,
IonionSails Team

[Yacht blocks from the booking system]
[Standard footer]

Notes for the sender: add the option sentence only if we place an option. The option length is in [Options](../policies/options.md) and the wording is in [Your option](your-option.md).

## Phrases cleaned

| Real email | In the approved text |
| --- | --- |
| Please do not hesitate to contact us. | If you have any questions, just reply to this email. |
| Thank you for your interest in our beauties! | Thank you for contacting IonionSails. |

| Our privately held yachts are in excellent condition and maintained all year round to make sure we offer you the best service. (bold) | Our yachts are privately owned and maintained all year round. (no bold) |
| Keep in mind that these special offers ... | The offer and our Free Cancellation terms are available for a limited time. |

The wording about ownership and maintenance differs between emails ("privately held", "privately owned", "own private shipyard"). See [D-033](../decisions/open.md#d-033).
