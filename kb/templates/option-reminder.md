# Option ends tomorrow (option reminder)

A three-line reminder that an option is about to expire. Sent to brokers, and to direct clients when they have an option.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | `IonionSails: Your Option ends tomorrow!`, or a reply in the thread `Re: IonionSails: Your Option on our [Boat]!` | brokers, direct clients | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r6836573897703654285) |
| When we send it | The day before the option expires. Other timings exist, see D-001. | all | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r846787662915164432) |
| Greeting | Dear Partners, (brokers) / Dear Mr./Mrs. [first name], (direct) | all | all | see examples |
| Length | 3 short sentences | all | all | see examples |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team (or the sender's name) | all | all | see examples |

## How we answer

Subject: Re: IonionSails: Your Option on our [Boat]!

Dear [Partners / first name],

Your option on our [Boat] for [dd.mm.yyyy to dd.mm.yyyy] expires tomorrow.

If you want to keep the yacht, let me know today and I will confirm the booking. If not, the yacht goes back on sale after the option ends.

Best regards,
IonionSails Team

Optional extra line, only when the team agrees to extend: I can extend the option once if you need more time. The rules for extending are in [Options](../policies/options.md).

## Phrases cleaned

| Real email | In the approved text |
| --- | --- |
| I trust this email finds you well. | Removed. |
| I just wanted to inform you that your option ... will expire tomorrow. | Your option ... expires tomorrow. |
| Should you require further details or assistance, feel free to reach out. | If you want to keep the yacht, let me know today. |

The second sentence ("goes back on sale") is added so the client knows the next step. It is not in the real emails. Please confirm it is fine to say.
