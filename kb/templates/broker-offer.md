# Broker offer

Our reply to a broker or agency who asks for availability and prices. A few lines of text, then the yacht blocks from the booking system with the commission shown.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject | The broker's own subject (`Re: [broker subject]`) | brokers | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874586982186023605) |
| When we send it | When a broker asks for a boat, dates or a price. | brokers, agencies | all | see examples |
| Greeting | Dear Partners, / Hello Partners, / Dear [contact first name], Greek brokers: Καλησπέρα [name], | brokers | all | see examples |
| Weekly rule | We charter Saturday to Saturday. The offer is built on that. | all | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874403225693172018) |
| Commission line | Shown in each yacht block. The rate and how it is calculated live on [Commission and invoices](../brokers/commission-and-invoices.md). Different rates appear in different emails. | brokers | all | see D-011 |
| Crewed yacht | Skipper and hostess fees as a short list, "excluding VAT and food", plus the list of what the yacht includes. Amounts live on [Crew](../policies/crew.md). | crewed catamarans | all | [email](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874259014759821720) |
| Footer | Same standard footer as [Our offer](our-offer.md) | all | all | see examples |
| Sign-off | Best regards, IonionSails Team (or the sender's name) | all | all | see examples |

Other short broker replies:

| Situation | Approved text |
| --- | --- |
| Fully booked | Thank you for your email. We are fully booked for the requested dates. We can check other dates if that helps. |
| Booking received | Thank you for your booking. We will send you the confirmation documents in a separate email shortly. |
| Payment received, balance still due | We have received the deposit payment, thank you. When the balance arrives, I will tell you so you can issue your commission invoice. |

## How we answer

Subject: Re: [broker subject]

Dear Partners,

Thank you for your inquiry. Please find our offer below for [Boat(s)], week commencing [Date].

We charter Saturday to Saturday, so the offer is built on that. The price breakdown includes the extras you asked for.

For a crewed yacht add: [Boat] is offered with a skipper and a hostess only. Fees: [skipper fee] and [hostess fee], both excluding VAT and food.

I can place an option for you if you tell me which yacht. The option length is on [Options](../policies/options.md).

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Best regards,
IonionSails Team

[Yacht blocks with commission and total price]
[Standard footer]
