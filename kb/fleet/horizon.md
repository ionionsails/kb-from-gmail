## How we answer

Horizon is a Leopard 43 PC (power catamaran) from 2019, based in Lefkada (Port of Lefkas). She has 4 double cabins and 10 berths (8 in cabins + 1 saloon; licensed for 10). Equipment: A/C in every cabin, water maker, generator, flexible start day. She is offered skippered only.
