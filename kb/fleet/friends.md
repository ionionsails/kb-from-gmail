- Offered only with a permanent skipper because the yacht is insured only for chartering with a skipper.

- Two dedicated skippers handle her. The crew profile is shared no later than one week before the charter. No direct skipper contact in advance, because crew may change.
- No hostess with 7 guests (no suitable sleeping place).

## How we answer

Friends is a Bavaria C57 from 2023, based in Lefkada. She has 5 cabins (master + 2 double + 1 bunk + 1 bow cabin used by the skipper) and 10 berths (8 guests + skipper). Equipment: A/C in every cabin and saloon, generator, bowthruster, hydraulic gangway, electric winch, TV, Wi-Fi, dishwasher, flexible start day. She is offered skippered only.
