## How we answer

Kalm is a Sun Odyssey 490 from 2020, based in Lefkada. She has 5 cabins (4 double + 1 bow) and 12 berths. Equipment: A/C in the saloon, generator, solar, bowthruster, 2 interior fridges, skipper cabin, full batten mainsail.
