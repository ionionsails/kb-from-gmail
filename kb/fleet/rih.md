## How we answer

Rih is a Fountaine Pajot Isla 40 from 2021, based in Lefkada. She has 5 cabins (4 double + 1 bow) and 10 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin and saloon, generator, lithium batteries, solar panel, 2 fridges, teak cockpit, convertible table, XL bimini, electric winch.
