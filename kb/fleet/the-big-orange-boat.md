## How we answer

The Big Orange Boat is a Bali 4.8 from 2024, based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 with bunk beds) and 12 berths (up to 10 guests). Equipment: flybridge saloon, A/C in every cabin, 2 generators, water maker, lithium batteries, projector and screen, hydraulic gangway. She is offered crewed only.
