Selling points and cautions:

- Navionics on the electronics, Imray paper charts on board. No chartplotter that takes charts from a tablet over Wi-Fi (ActiveCaptain).
- Our example of A/C in every cabin plus a generator: A/C usable through the whole yacht on hot August days.
- Website: Described as the perfect 5-cabin yacht for Ionian getaways

## How we answer

Atman is a Oceanis 51.1 from 2021, based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 1 bunk + 1 bow) and 12 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, lithium batteries, solar, bowthruster, 2 fridges, electric winch, cockpit cushions, skipper cabin, furling mainsail.
