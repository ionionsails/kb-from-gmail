## How we answer

Breathless II is a day cruise boat (Bavaria Sport 300 (motor boat), 2015, 30 ft) from Port of Lefkas. She takes up to 10 guests including the skipper. The day runs from 09:00 to 18:00. The price includes cleaning and a welcome pack with local products and towels. Fuel is extra and is calculated on the actual consumption. Please contact us about one month before the trip.
