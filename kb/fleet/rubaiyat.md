## How we answer

Rubaiyat is a Bali 4.2 from 2025 (brand new), based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 bow) and 10 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin and saloon, generator, water maker, solar, full batten mainsail, speakers, USB in every cabin, XL bimini, household-style fridge, lifting aft door between saloon and cockpit.

## Open questions

- Website vs emails: Rubaiyat cabins. Website text says 4 cabins, emails and the website fleet list say 6 (4 double + 2 bow) ([D-038](../decisions/open.md#d-038))
- Website vs emails: skipper fee. Website says 200 EUR per day + meals, emails say 1,470 EUR per week excl. VAT and provisioning, no day skipper, see [Crew](../policies/crew.md). The website value is probably outdated ([D-025](../decisions/open.md#d-025))
