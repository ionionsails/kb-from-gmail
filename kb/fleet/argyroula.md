## How we answer

Argyroula is a Oceanis 41.1 from 2020, based in Lefkada. She has 3 double cabins and 8 berths (6 in cabins + 2 in saloon). Equipment: bowthruster, solar, A/C in the saloon, furling mainsail, speakers, USB in every cabin, e-check-in.
