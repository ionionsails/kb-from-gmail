## How we answer

Imeroessa is a Lagoon 40 from 2022, based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 bow) in most offers, 5 in two older ones and 10 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, lithium batteries, solar, electric winch, 2 fridges with a small freezer, teak cockpit and steps, full batten mainsail.
