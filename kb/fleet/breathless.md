## How we answer

Breathless is a Bavaria Cruiser 56 from 2016 (built Nov 2015, rebuilt 2020), based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 1 bunk + 1 bow) and 12 berths. Equipment: bowthruster, generator, A/C in every cabin, solar, 2 fridges, cockpit cushions, safety lines on both sides, furling mainsail, 2 wheels, steady gangway, skipper cabin.
