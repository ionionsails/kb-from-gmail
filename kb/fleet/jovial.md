## How we answer

Jovial is a Oceanis 51.1 from 2019, based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 1 bunk + 1 bow) and 12 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, solar, bowthruster, fridge and freezer, furling mainsail.

## Open questions

- Website vs emails: Jovial cabins. Website text says 5 cabins, emails and the website fleet list say 6 ([D-038](../decisions/open.md#d-038))
- Website vs emails: skipper fee. Website says 200 EUR per day + meals, emails say 1,470 EUR per week excl. VAT and provisioning, no day skipper, see [Crew](../policies/crew.md). The website value is probably outdated ([D-025](../decisions/open.md#d-025))
