## How we answer

Aspromonte is a Bali 4.4 from 2024 (built Dec 2023), based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 bow) and 10 berths sold (registered for 12). Equipment: A/C in every cabin and saloon with its own panel per cabin, generator, water maker (about 200 l/h), solar, flybridge, forward lounge, large aft opening to the saloon, family fridge with ice maker, electric toilets.
