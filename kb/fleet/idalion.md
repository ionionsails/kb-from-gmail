## How we answer

Idalion is a Lagoon 42 from 2021 (built Nov 2020), based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 bow) and 12 berths (10 in the latest email). Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, lithium batteries, water maker, hydraulic gangway, teak cockpit, full batten mainsail, 2 skipper cabins; the saloon table converts to a double.
