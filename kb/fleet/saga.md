## How we answer

Saga is a Lagoon 52F from 2019 (built Nov 2018), refit 2026, based in Lefkada. She has 7 cabins (4 double incl. VIP master + 1 bunk crew cabin + 2 front) and 10 berths (8 guests + 2 crew). Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, solar, water maker, full batten mainsail. She is offered crewed only.
