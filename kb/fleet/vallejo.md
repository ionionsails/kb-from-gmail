## How we answer

Vallejo is a Bali 4.0 from 2018 (built Nov 2017, rebuilt 2020), based in Lefkada. She has 5 cabins (4 double + 1 bow) and 10 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, lithium batteries, solar panel, full batten mainsail.
