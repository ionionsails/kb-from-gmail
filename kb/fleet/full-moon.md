## How we answer

Full Moon is a Lagoon 42 from 2019, based in Lefkada. She has 6 cabins (4 double + 2 bow) and 12 berths. Equipment: A/C in every cabin, generator, lithium batteries, 2 skipper cabins.

## Open questions

- Website vs emails: Full Moon cabins. Website text says 4 cabins, emails say 6 (4 double + 2 bow) ([D-038](../decisions/open.md#d-038))
- Website vs emails: skipper fee. Website says 200 EUR per day + meals, emails say 1,470 EUR per week excl. VAT and provisioning, no day skipper, see [Crew](../policies/crew.md). The website value is probably outdated ([D-025](../decisions/open.md#d-025))
