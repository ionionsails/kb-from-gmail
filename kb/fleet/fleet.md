## How we answer

Which boats do you have: "In Lefkada we have catamarans from 40 to 46 ft and monohulls from 38 to 53 ft, and two crewed yachts, The Big Orange Boat and Saga. In the Saronic we have six boats at Nea Peramos. Tell me your dates and group size and I will check availability."

Why our yachts: "All our yachts are maintained over winter in our own shipyard by an in-house technical team, which also supports the fleet during the season. Each yacht in our offer has a video, a page with real photos and an e-check-in link." Use this only for our own boats, not partner boats. Catamarans have more amenities and a more relaxed feel. A monohull is more engaging for beginners learning the basics.

Ownership wording (privately held, privately owned, or "we own our boats"): not decided, see [D-033](../decisions/open.md#d-033).
