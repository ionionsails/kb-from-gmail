## How we answer

Barbagiannis is a Bavaria C45 from 2021 (Dec 2020), based in Lefkada. She has 4 cabins (front cabin convertible, 3 or 4 cabin version) and 10 berths. Equipment: bowthruster, solar, A/C in the saloon, lithium batteries, TV, 2 fridges, teak cockpit, furling mainsail.
