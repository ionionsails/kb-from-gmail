# Winter base (Nea Peramos Shipyard)

Our yachts are maintained over winter in our own drydock area at the Nea Peramos Shipyard near Athens, from October to April.

## Rules

| Item | Value | Applies to | Season | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shipyard | Exclusive cooperation since 2014 with the Nea Peramos Shipyard, which has run for almost 100 years. Part of its area is our winter base and drydock point | Nea Peramos | Oct to Apr | [website](https://ionionsails.com/our-bases/winter-base/) |
| Work done | Maintenance of owned and managed yachts, every day from October to April. Winterising of yachts up to 25 m. The yard is licensed to build and maintain yachts up to 25 m | Nea Peramos | Oct to Apr | [website](https://ionionsails.com/our-bases/winter-base/) |

## How we answer

Winter maintenance:

> Our yachts are maintained over winter in our own shipyard near Athens by our in-house technical team. The same team supports the fleet during the season.

Use this for our own boats only, not for partner boats.
