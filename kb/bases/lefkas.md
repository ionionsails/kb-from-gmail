# Lefkas base (Ionian)

Our main and older base, in the Port of Lefkas, with two private pontoons and offices. Brand IonionSails. Season runs from late April to mid October.

## How we answer

Delivery and docks:

> Our docks do not have individual numbers. For delivery, please use the name "IonionSails Private Docks – Port of Lefkas". The port may also appear as Lefkas Public Marina. The attached base brochure shows the exact location.

Airport:

> The nearest airport is Aktion (PVK), about 30 to 40 minutes by car from our base in Lefkada. If you cannot find suitable flights, you can fly to Athens. The drive takes about 3.5 to 4 hours, and we can arrange a private transfer.

Visiting boat:

> You may moor your boat at one of our docks. It is first come, first served, so you can choose any free spot when you arrive. We provide mooring lines. Electricity and water are EUR 35 per day, which you can settle at our office. The office opens at 09:00.

Season end and 2027:

> This week is the last operational week of the season. Our Lefkada base will reopen on 24 April 2027.
