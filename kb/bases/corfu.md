# Corfu base (Gouvia Marina, website only)

The website lists a Corfu base at Gouvia Marina. In our emails we say we have no base in Corfu, so use this page only as what we publish, not as what we offer.

## How we answer

Corfu start:

> We do not have a base in Corfu, and we do not offer a start there. You can start in Lefkada and sail north, or take the ferry to Igoumenitsa and drive to Lefkada (3 to 3.5 hours).
