# Crewed yacht charters

A crewed charter has a captain and a hostess on board, and a cook on request. Fees and duties are on [Crew](../policies/crew.md).

## How we answer

Crewed charter:

> On a crewed charter you have a captain and a hostess on board, and a cook can be added. The hostess looks after shopping, breakfast, lunch and the common areas. Tell me your dates and group size, and I will check which crewed yachts are available and send an offer.
