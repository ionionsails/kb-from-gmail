# Bareboat charters

A bareboat charter is for experienced sailors who handle the yacht themselves. One qualified skipper and one more experienced crew member are needed, or we provide a skipper.

## How we answer

Bareboat:

> A bareboat charter means you sail the yacht yourselves. We need one skipper with a valid licence and a second person with sailing experience. If you do not have the licence, we can provide one of our skippers. Tell me your dates and group size, and I will check availability and send you an offer.
