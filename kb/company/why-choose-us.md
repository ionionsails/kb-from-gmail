## How we answer

Why IonionSails:

> We run our own base in Lefkada with our own technical team, and our yachts are maintained over winter in our own shipyard. Each yacht has real photos, a video and an e-check-in link. During your charter our team is available 24/7. We are members of SITESAP and hold a brokering licence from EOT.

Use the shipyard sentence for our own boats only, not for partner boats.
