# Sailing area from Lefkas (overview)

From Lefkas the nearby islands are a few hours away. The eastern Ionian is weather protected, sunny most days and windy enough for sailing, which is what we say to clients.

## How we answer

Where can we sail:

> From Lefkas you can sail to Meganisi, Kalamos, Kastos, Ithaca and Kefalonia, which are a few hours away. Further north are Paxos, Antipaxos, Parga and Syvota. Zakynthos is possible with planning. You agree the route with your skipper on embarkation day, depending on the weather. I can send you our Ionian Sailing Guide and suggested itineraries.
