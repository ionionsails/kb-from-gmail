# Kefalonia

Kefalonia is about 3.5 hours of sailing from Lefkada. Shelter differs a lot between ports, so check the wind before choosing.

## Rules

| Place | What the site says | Shelter or caution | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fiskardo (north) | Famous meeting point for charter yachts. Old harbour and local taverns | | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/kefalonia/) |
| Agia Effimia | Port designated for charter yachts from Lefkada | Bay protected in any weather. Care needed on entry | same |
| Sami | Bay safe in winds from north round to east. Cave of Melissani nearby | North to east winds | same |
| Antisamos | Next to Sami. Organised beach | | same |
| Argostoli | Shelter in all conditions except north winds | Not for north winds | same |
| Poros | Overnight mooring is advised against | Sudden NW wind and swell | same |
| Assos | Least protected shelter on Kefalonia | Unpredictable wind changes | same |
| Foki bay (north) | Named after seals | | same |
| Agia Sofia (east) | Quiet beach, good overnight spot with easy mooring | | same |
| Sailing time from Lefkada | About 3.5 hours | | same |

Ferry from Lefkada Town to Sami: about 3.5 hours (see [Lefkas base](../bases/lefkas.md)).

## How we answer

Kefalonia:

> Kefalonia is about 3.5 hours of sailing from Lefkada. Fiskardo in the north is the favourite stop for charter yachts, and Agia Effimia has a port for our yachts with good protection. Poros and Assos are not good for overnight stays when the wind turns. Your skipper will pick the best port for the day.
