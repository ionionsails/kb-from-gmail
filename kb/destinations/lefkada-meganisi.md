# Lefkada coast and Meganisi

Stops around our home island and the island next to it, with what the website says about each.

## Rules

### Lefkada

| Place | What the site says | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nikiana | Small fish harbour, first bay south of the base | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/lefkada/) |
| Nidri | Cosmopolitan centre with a pedestrian street and promenade | same |
| Vasiliki | Largest bay in south Lefkada, known for windsurfing winds | same |
| Poros (south east) | Well protected from south winds, white pebble beach | same |
| Large south east bay | Buoyed swimming area. Anchor outside it in 12 to 15 m. Prevailing wind gusts out of the bay | same |

## How we answer

Near Lefkas:

> Meganisi is the usual first stop, about 11 nautical miles from Lefkas. Vathi is a large natural harbour, and there are good swimming bays on the island. On the west side anchoring is harder, so your skipper will choose with the weather.
