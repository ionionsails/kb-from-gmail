# Zakynthos

Zakynthos is south of our area and possible with planning. Check weather and timing with the skipper first.

## How we answer

Zakynthos:

> Zakynthos is possible from Lefkas, but it needs planning because of the distance. The shipwreck beach can only be reached by boat and is best early in the morning or late afternoon. Let us know your dates and we will check the route with the skipper.
