# North of Lefkas: Paxoi, Antipaxos, Corfu and the mainland

Paxoi, Antipaxos, Corfu and the mainland ports are the northern part of our area. Longer legs, so plan more days.

## Rules

| Place | What the site says | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gaios | Main port, east coast, the capital | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/paxoi/) |

## How we answer

Going north:

> Paxos and Antipaxos are the highlight of the north. Gaios is the main port, Laka is a round natural harbour, and Voutoumi on Antipaxos is the bay we recommend for the night. Parga and Syvota on the mainland make good stops on the way. We have no base in Corfu, so we suggest starting in Lefkada and sailing north.
