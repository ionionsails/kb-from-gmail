# Kalamos, Kastos and Atokos

Quiet islands close to Lefkas, good for swim stops and calm nights.

## Rules

| Place | What the site says | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kalamos | In the inner Ionian marine protected area. Pine covered, small deserted sand and pebble beaches. The page calls it a must visit | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/kalamos/) |
| Kalamos village | Fishing village. Daily ferries from Mytikas on the mainland | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/kalamos/), [mainland](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/mainland/) |
| Asprogiali | Two small beaches in a pine forest | same |
| South bay, named by the Venetians | Remains of an abandoned old village, a favourite of boaters | same |
| Formikola islet | Reachable only by sea. Home of the Mediterranean seal | same |
| Kastos | Between Kalamos and the mainland, up to 1 km wide. Rocky west side. Sand and pebble beaches on the east side with safe mooring | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/kastos/) |
| Kastos village | A few taverns and a supermarket (itinerary page) | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/kastos/) |
| Wast Bay (Kastos) | Recommended quick swim stop | same |
| Atokos | 5 miles south west of Kastos. Private island owned by the Tsakos shipowning family, uninhabited, in the NATURA 2000 area. Visitors may visit and swim | [website](https://ionionsails.com/attraction/sailing-from-lefkas/atokos/) |
| Atokos, One House Bay | Easy. 5 m depth, sandy bottom. Not protected from east winds | same |
| Atokos, Cliff Bay | Medium difficulty. Anchoring is difficult, about 20 m depth. Not protected from south winds. Visit in good weather | same |

## How we answer

Quiet islands:

> Kalamos and Kastos are the quiet side of our area. Kalamos is pine covered with small empty beaches, and Kastos has a small village with taverns and safe mooring on the east side. Atokos is private and uninhabited, and you can stop there to swim in good weather.
