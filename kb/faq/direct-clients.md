# FAQ for direct clients

What direct clients ask us most and our approved answer; values live on the linked pages. Most asked first; "Asked" = emails in the pilot. Brokers: [FAQ for brokers](brokers.md).

## Questions

### Is there an early booking discount, and what is the final price?
Yes. Our offers show the list price, then the early booking or special week discount, then an extra IonionSails discount (SaronicSails at the Athens base). Two-week charters, several yachts and returning guests can get a further step. Rates change with demand, so an offer is valid only when we send it. See [Discounts](../policies/discounts.md).
Asked: 119. Example: [offer with discount stack](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r-1874254210341252282)

### How does the security deposit work, and what is the Damage Waiver?
The deposit is a card pre-authorisation, not a charge. Option A: your own deposit insurance, arranged before arrival; the yacht's full deposit is then held on a card at check-in. Option B: the Damage Waiver at check-in, a non-refundable fee plus a smaller refundable amount held on a card, which you can insure on the spot. You can book now and choose later. Amounts per boat are on [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md).
Asked: 90. Example: [Option A and B explained](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r6687077659634441532)

### What does a skipper, hostess or cook cost, and what is APA?
The skipper fee is per week, excluding VAT and meals; crewed-only yachts have their own crew fees. You include the skipper in your meals on board or give him a small daily allowance ashore. Crew fees are paid on arrival. Crewed yachts need an APA for fuel, food, supplies and marina fees; the crew keeps receipts and returns the unused balance. See [Crew](../policies/crew.md).
Asked: 79. Example: [skipper fee and APA](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1878498610281136452)

### We want to book. What happens next, and when is the booking confirmed?
Thank you for your booking. We send the booking documents in a separate email with a payment link. The booking is secured once the first payment is in our account. See [Booking documents](../policies/booking-documents.md).
Asked: 60. Example: [booking acknowledged](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r8085554789230008228)

### Can we charter mid-week or for fewer days? When do we board and return?
Our charters run weekly, Saturday to Saturday. You can use the yacht for fewer days, but the full week is reserved and paid. The yacht returns to base on Friday afternoon, you sleep on board the last night and check out on Saturday morning. Times are on [Check-in](../policies/check-in.md).
Asked: 53. Example: [weekly basis](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1876961362422346354)

### Which extras are obligatory, and what do the optional extras cost?
The end cleaning (Start Pack) is obligatory; the amount depends on boat size. Everything else is optional and paid at the base on arrival. Extras can be added later; we send updated documents. Prices are on [Extras](../policies/extras.md).
Asked: 51. Example: [extras list in offer](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1875419449457388182)

### Which airport do we fly to, and can you arrange a transfer?
For Lefkada the nearest airport is Preveza (Aktion, PVK); from Athens it is about 3.5 to 4 hours by road. We do not run our own shuttle. We book your transfer with our transfer partner once you send the transfer form. For Nea Peramos see the train question below. Rates are on [Transfers](../policies/transfers.md).
Asked: 48. Example: [PVK or Athens](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1879212509309108115)

### Is the yacht equipment suitable (BBQ, coffee, speakers, sails, water)?
Specs per boat are on the [fleet pages](../fleet/fleet.md). We do not add a BBQ if the yacht has no factory-fitted one, for safety. A Nespresso machine and a portable speaker are optional extras. We do not offer gennakers or Code Zero. Tank water is not certified as drinking water, so bring bottled water.

### What do you still need from us before the charter?
The crew list (Boarding Pass link or Excel form, not PDF), a copy of the skipper's licence, the charterer's address and phone number, your arrival time, your deposit option and, if needed, the transfer form. Deadlines are on [Booking documents](../policies/booking-documents.md).

### How much do we pay, when, and how?
The payment plan covers the charter fee only; extras are paid at the base. The first half is due within 7 days, the balance before the charter. You pay by bank transfer with the booking confirmation number as reference, or by a secure payment link. The charter fee cannot be paid fully in cash. See [Payment](../policies/payment.md).

Yes, from Competent Crew up to Yachtmaster, mainly from our Nea Peramos base, plus private instructor weeks for groups. Coastal Skipper needs night sailing and skipper experience; without it we suggest Day Skipper first. Prices and dates are on [Licences](../policies/licences.md). Two-week package for February 2027: not found – please confirm.

### Can you hold the yacht while we decide?
Yes. Send us the yacht and exact dates and we place a free, non-binding option and send the option confirmation. It can be extended on request. See [Options](../policies/options.md).

### Something was wrong on our charter. What do you offer?
During the charter, report it straight away so our team can fix it. After the charter we review each point with the team and reply point by point. A goodwill gesture is a voucher for a future charter, not a cash refund. See [Complaints](../policies/complaints.md).

### The yacht is at the other base. Can you move it?
Normally no. Guests travel to the yacht's base and we arrange a transfer. In one special case we delivered a yacht Lefkada to Nea Peramos with two of our skippers, fuel and the Corinth Canal fee at cost. See [Nea Peramos](../bases/nea-peramos.md).

### How does the skipper work? Can we choose him or get his number?
The skipper is responsible for the yacht; you plan the route together, weather permitting, and you can help sail. We do not share skipper contacts in advance, and there is no one-day skipper. A tip is optional. See [Crew](../policies/crew.md).
Asked: 29. Example: [skipper details](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1873306375728090604)

### Is the outboard engine included?
The standard dinghy is included. The outboard is a paid extra and not free of charge as standard. Catamarans can take a larger dinghy with a stronger outboard. Prices are on [Extras](../policies/extras.md).

### Is the boat safe for children?
All yachts have harnesses and tethers, including for children, and jack lines. The skipper briefs everyone on safety at the start. A kids safety net with children's life jackets is an optional extra. See [Extras](../policies/extras.md).

### Lost items and small requests (hotels, car rental, Zakynthos)
Found items wait at our office for pick-up. We can suggest hotels and car rental near the base. We do not arrange third-party payments or our own transfers to other islands.

### Can we bring or rent a SUP, kayak or other water toys?
You can bring your own SUP or rent one from us; kayaks and a seascooter are also available. We do not have an e-foil. Prices are on [Extras](../policies/extras.md).

### Can we board earlier, or arrive late on Saturday?
You can board earlier if the yacht is ready; guaranteed early boarding is the paid Early Check-in. If you land late, you board Saturday evening and we do the paperwork on Sunday morning. See [Check-in](../policies/check-in.md).

### When do we get the deposit back? Can we pay it in cash?
We release the hold after return and checks; your bank then takes some working days, and we send a release letter. At least part of the deposit must be on a card; the rest can be cash only with own deposit insurance. See [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md).

### Do we still need the Damage Waiver if we take your skipper?
No. With our professional skipper the deposit is halved and held on a guest's card at check-in. You stay responsible for the interior, equipment and items lost or damaged by guests. If you are named main skipper, the full deposit applies. See [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md).
Asked: 15. Example: [skippered deposit](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r2016699508300997895)

### Is my licence accepted for a bareboat charter?
One person on board needs a valid sailing licence; ICC valid for coastal waters is accepted. A motor-only licence is not valid for a sailing yacht or sailing catamaran. Send a copy of front and back and we check it. If no one qualifies, take a skipper from our team; then no licence is needed. See [Licences](../policies/licences.md).

### What if we have to cancel or change dates?
The cancellation terms are on [Cancellation](../policies/cancellation.md): full refund up to a set number of days before embarkation, then a credit only if the yacht is rebooked on the same terms. Moving dates or yacht is normally not possible; airport closures are assessed case by case.

### Which bases do you have?
Two: Lefkada for the Ionian and Nea Peramos near Athens for the Saronic Gulf (SaronicSails). We have no base in Corfu, Skiathos or Volos; we can check partner boats. See [Lefkas](../bases/lefkas.md) and [Nea Peramos](../bases/nea-peramos.md).

### What is included in the price? Is VAT included?
The yacht price includes VAT, gas, water, dinghy, linen and towels and harbour fees for the first and last night. Fuel, other mooring fees and provisioning are extra. There is no tourist tax on boats. See [Extras](../policies/extras.md).

For charters from Nea Peramos we give free one-way high-speed train tickets between Athens Airport and the base, for all passengers. Send full names and your preferred time slot and we send the tickets. See [Transfers](../policies/transfers.md).

### What insurance do we need, and what does the Damage Waiver not cover?
Our only requirement is the security deposit; travel insurance is up to you. The Damage Waiver does not cover blocked toilets or gross negligence. We send the Terms and Conditions on request. See [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md).
