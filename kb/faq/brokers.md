# FAQ for brokers

What brokers and agencies ask us most and our approved answer; values live on the linked pages. Most asked first; "Asked" = emails in the pilot. Direct clients: [FAQ for direct clients](direct-clients.md).

## Questions

### Can you place an option for our client? How long does it last, and can it be extended?
Yes. We place the option in Booking Manager and send the option confirmation; please check the expiry date on it. We extend on request, and we remind you before it expires. If the yacht already has an option, the first holder has priority: we ask them to confirm and meanwhile suggest a similar yacht or week. See [Options](../policies/options.md).
Asked: 118. Examples: [option placed](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1876749642158642025), [extension](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1879491529368354719)

### Do you have a yacht for these dates? Please send an offer.
Thank you for your interest in our fleet. Please tell us bareboat or crewed, the number of guests and the dates. Charters are weekly, Saturday to Saturday. The offer shows price, discount, commission, net total, obligatory extras and deposit, and is valid only when sent. Crewed-only yachts cannot go bareboat, and we offer a boat through one channel per booking. See [Working with brokers](../brokers/working-with-brokers.md) and [Fleet](../fleet/fleet.md).
Asked: 103. Example: [clarifying the request](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1878936898825941428)

### What commission do we get, and how is the net price calculated?
The commission is fixed by yacht type and cannot be changed; we have no KPI or higher-commission scheme. Discounts apply first, then commission. If you cannot issue a commission invoice, we can quote a net package instead. The client's confirmation never shows commission. See [Commission and invoices](../brokers/commission-and-invoices.md).
Asked: 87. Example: [commission fixed](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1876391812016631910)

### Who do we address the commission invoice to, and when do you need it?
To the boat ownership company of that yacht, not to IonionSails or the guest; the details are on the charter party and the boarding pass. We need it before embarkation and in the same month it is issued. Without it the charter party shows the net price. See [Commission and invoices](../brokers/commission-and-invoices.md).
Asked: 87. Example: [invoice rules](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1879562259882109869)

### Who collects the client's payment, and when is our commission paid?
The client pays us directly. We accept the booking once the payment is in our account; until then the yacht stays on option. We pay your commission after the client payment and your invoice; the first instalment can be set to cover your full commission. You cannot pay on behalf of the client. See [Broker payments](../brokers/broker-payments.md).
Asked: 69. Example: [client pays us](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874777414976696702)

### We sent the payment. Can you confirm the booking?
Thank you for the proof of payment. We confirm as soon as the funds show in our account; international and weekend transfers can take a couple of days. Please use the booking confirmation number as reference. See [Broker payments](../brokers/broker-payments.md).
Asked: 56. Example: [payment confirmation](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1873031055729329837)

### Can you give an extra discount or a free extra?
You may give the client up to 5% extra discount from your own commission; this is the maximum for everybody. Please tell us the price you offered first. Our rates are usually already very competitive; a free extra such as the outboard is case by case. Early check-in is never free. See [Discounts](../policies/discounts.md).
Asked: 54. Example: [5% from commission](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874587154664108854)

### What are the payment and cancellation terms for broker bookings?
The same as for direct clients: half within 7 days of confirmation, the balance before the charter, charter fee only; extras are paid at the base. Short-notice terms: [D-005](../decisions/open.md#d-005). Cancellation follows our standard terms with the rebooking credit. See [Payment](../policies/payment.md) and [Cancellation](../policies/cancellation.md).
Asked: 51. Example: [terms for broker](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1879454652247709947)

### What documents do you send, and what do you still need?
After payment we send, in a separate email, the broker confirmation (with commission), the client copy (without), the charter party, the boarding pass with crew list link and the base info. We need the charterer's address and contact, arrival time, crew list, licence copy and the guests' deposit choice. See [Booking documents](../policies/booking-documents.md).
Asked: 34. Example: [missing details](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1878932062067059696)

### Can the client pay by card? Is American Express accepted?
Yes, we send a secure payment link on request; it is valid for a few days and we send a new one if it expires. Otherwise bank transfer to the account on the booking confirmation. Do not confirm American Express in writing until we check. See [Payment](../policies/payment.md).
Asked: 30. Example: [payment link](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r7701694780194056468)

### Our guests reported problems. What compensation do you offer?
We reply quickly, check each yacht and report cause and fix point by point, including whether the item was in the original offer. Goodwill is a voucher, not a cash refund; an agency voucher is split between guests or checked with us before use. See [Complaints](../policies/complaints.md).
Asked: 30. Example: [defect report reply](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874507425924845624)

### The payment is late or short. What now?
We remind you, then set a deadline and ask you to confirm the booking is still valid, or we cancel. A short delay can be accepted. For a short amount please send the bank slip. No embarkation without full payment. See [Payment](../policies/payment.md).
Asked: 26. Example: [overdue follow-up](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-a:r8321530821863526318)

### How do APA and crew fees work on crewed yachts?
Yacht prices include VAT. The APA covers fuel, food, supplies and marina fees: part by bank transfer in advance, the rest on arrival; receipts are kept and the unused balance is returned. Crew fees and cleaning are paid on arrival. See [Crew](../policies/crew.md).
Asked: 20. Example: [APA terms](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1876300979224013293)

### Can the agency cover the security deposit or do the Damage Waiver?
No. The deposit is a card pre-authorisation on embarkation day; the cardholder must be at our office and sign. The Damage Waiver is also done only at our office, because any damage claim runs through us. See [Deposit and insurance](../policies/deposit-insurance.md).
Asked: 16. Example: [cardholder present](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1811271877700333038)

### The guests want to sail a skippered-only yacht bareboat, or change the charter party.
The yacht is insured only with a skipper, so no bareboat. "Bareboat" in the charter party is standard Greek wording. The charter party cannot be changed, but we can add a supplementary document; tell us exactly what needs amending. No refund when the skipper was shown in the documents.
Asked: 9. Example: [skippered-only](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1874486601547154337)

### We are a new partner. What are your terms?
The client pays us directly and we pay back your commission. You may advertise our boats with at most 5% off from your commission. We send our T&Cs and the charter party on request and usually set up an online meeting. See [Working with brokers](../brokers/working-with-brokers.md).
Asked: 4. Example: [partner terms](https://mail.google.com/mail/?authuser=info@ionionsails.com#all/thread-f:1811271877700333038)
