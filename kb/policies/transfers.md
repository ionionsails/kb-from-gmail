# Transfers and train to Nea Peramos

We do not run our own shuttle. Lefkas transfers are booked through an external partner and paid to the driver. For Nea Peramos charters we give free one-way high-speed train tickets.

## How we answer

Transfer from Preveza (Lefkas base):

> We do not run our own airport shuttle, but we work with a transport company and can arrange your transfer through them. The current rates from Preveza Airport to Lefkas, per way, are: taxi up to 4 passengers EUR 50, van for 5 to 8 passengers EUR 85, minibus up to 16 passengers EUR 150. These are this year's rates. If they change for next year, we will tell you when we confirm your transfer. You pay the driver directly.

Asking for details:

> To book it, please fill in the transfer form with your flight details, the number of people and the pick-up time. We will send it to the partner and confirm with you.

Free train (Nea Peramos):

> We offer free one-way high-speed train tickets between Athens Airport and our base in Nea Peramos, in either direction, for all passengers. Please send me the journey you need, the number of passengers, the full names and your preferred two-hour time slot. I will send the tickets by email or WhatsApp.

Free transfer request:

> We cannot provide the transfer free of charge, and we cannot lower the partner's prices.
