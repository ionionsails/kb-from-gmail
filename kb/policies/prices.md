# Prices and price levels

We do not quote a charter price from the wiki. The price depends on your dates, on availability and on the market, so we check availability and send an offer.

## How we answer

How much is a boat:

> Let me check availability for your dates and I will send you an offer. The price depends on the week and on availability. It includes VAT, gas, water, dinghy, linen and towels, and harbour fees for the first and last night.

Catamaran or monohull:

> A catamaran has more living space and is very stable. Most groups of six or more and families with children choose a catamaran. I will check both for your dates and send you an offer.
