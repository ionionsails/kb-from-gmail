# Prices and price levels

We do not quote a weekly charter price from the wiki. The price depends on dates and availability, so we check the dates and send an offer.

## How we answer

How much is a boat:

> The price depends on your dates and on availability. Let me check the boat for your week and send you an offer with the discounts that apply.

Catamaran or monohull:

> A catamaran costs more than a monohull, but it has more living space and is very stable. Most groups of six or more and families with children choose a catamaran.
