# Licences and RYA courses

On a bareboat charter one person on board must hold a valid sailing licence that the port authorities accept. With our professional skipper on board, no guest licence is needed.

Course rules:

- Typical week: Sunday 17:00 arrival and briefing, Monday to Friday sailing, Saturday final session and departure around midday.
- Without night sailing experience a client cannot go straight to Coastal Skipper. Offer Day Skipper first.
- Day Skipper without Competent Crew is possible if the student has the practical basics and does the theory first.
- Day Skipper and Coastal Skipper cannot run on two consecutive weeks. Day Skipper and Competent Crew can run on the same yacht in the same week.
- Main RYA base is Nea Peramos, run together with Ionian Mode. Ionian Mode also has a base in Sivota, Lefkada (availability to be checked).
- Course enquiries that reach the SaronicSails mailbox are forwarded to the training mailbox.
- Courses restart in spring 2027. The date is not settled, see D-035.

## How we answer

Licence accepted or not:

> Only one person on board needs to hold the sailing qualification to be the designated skipper. ICC certificates are accepted if they are valid for Coastal Waters. An ICC for Inland Waters only does not meet the requirements. Please send us a photo or PDF of the full licence, so we can check it with the port authorities.

Motor-only licence:

> Thank you for the details. Unfortunately a licence for motor yachts is not valid for operating a sailing yacht on a bareboat charter, even if you are certified for larger vessels. Does another member of your group hold a valid sailing licence for coastal waters? If so, send us a copy and we will review it. Otherwise we can offer a professional skipper from our team.

Our skipper on board:

> The yacht will be handled by the professional skipper assigned by our company, so we do not need a licence copy from your guest.

Licence copy timing:

> We need the licence copy 7 days before arrival, so the port authorities have enough time. Please make sure it is valid and acceptable to them.
