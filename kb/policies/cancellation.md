# Cancellation, date changes and rebooking

Cancelling 120 days or more before embarkation: the advance payment is refunded in full. Less than 120 days: the amount paid is kept, unless the yacht is rebooked on exactly the same terms.

## How we answer

Cancellation terms:

> Up to 120 days before embarkation, the advance payment is fully refunded. Within 120 days, the amount paid is retained. If the yacht is rebooked by another client under the exact same terms, the amount paid becomes a credit for a future charter. The credit must be used for a charter starting no later than 30 October of the following year. It is not transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash.

Client asks to change the dates:

> Reservations normally cannot be moved to other dates. Let me check what is possible for your booking and get back to you.

Client asks to change the charter party:

> The standard charter party cannot be modified. If you need to add a specific point, we can put it in a supplementary document.
