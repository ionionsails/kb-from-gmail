# Options (non-binding holds)

An option is a free, non-binding hold on a yacht while the client decides. Standard length is 5 days, with an extension on request. The length and the reminder are not yet decided.

Brokers: see [working-with-brokers.md](../brokers/working-with-brokers.md).

## How we answer

What is an option:

> An option is a temporary reservation. It holds the yacht for you while you finalize your plans. It is free and does not commit you to anything.

Option confirmed:

> Please find attached the option confirmation for your yacht. The option is valid for 5 days. If you need more time, let me know before it expires.

Client wants to wait longer:

> I can extend the option on request. Let me check that no other client is waiting for the same yacht and get back to you.

Prices may change:

> Prices and availability are valid at the time of this email. If you like the offer, I can place an option so the yacht stays free for you.
