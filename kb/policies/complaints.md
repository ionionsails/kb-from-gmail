# Complaints and problems during a charter

We acknowledge the same day, review internally, then answer point by point. Goodwill is a voucher, not cash, and there is no standard amount: every case so far was decided on its own.

### Goodwill cases seen

These are cases, not a policy. Do not quote one as our standard.

| Date | Case | Who | Gesture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-08-27 | Dinghy outboard and solar panel. Guests used 166 litres of fuel, 385 EUR | Broker | 400 EUR fuel reimbursement |
| 2026-08-31 | Cleaning, cushions, early check-in not delivered | Broker | Early check-in fee refunded |
| 2026-09-01 | Bali 4.4 defects | Broker | Choice of a 1,000 EUR voucher for the next charter booked through the agency, or a free one-way charter Lefkada to Athens (Oct 2026 or Apr 2027, guests pay only cleaning and operating costs) |
| 2026-09-07 to 09-22 | Same case, settled | Broker | Two 500 EUR vouchers, deducted from amounts owed to us. The first voucher is not for the original guests. Broker checks with us before offering it |
| 2026-09-23 | Rubaiyat, several points reviewed one by one | Direct guest | 400 EUR voucher toward a future charter |
| 2026-10-14 | A partner base's 200 EUR deposit-waiver voucher does not generally apply to us | Direct guest | 200 EUR voucher toward a future charter |

Internal only, never for the customer: one team member thought 1,000 EUR was high and could turn into cash payouts. The case guests implicitly asked about was two lost days, net price about 2,400 EUR.

### Common technical answers

| Issue | Answer | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment not advertised (e.g. inverter) | Check the offer first. Vallejo was not advertised with an inverter, only the fridge socket runs on it | Vallejo |
| Tripped circuit breaker | Watermaker plus all air-conditioning on the generator can trip it. Reset it | Catamarans with generator |
| Waste system | Valve must not be fully opened, water comes in through the inlet. The technician explains the correct use | All |
| Anchor windlass | Use the engine to move the boat toward the anchor. The windlass lifts anchor and chain vertically and is not made to pull the boat | All |
| Outboard with no fuel | Replacement outboard provided and tested with the guests | Full Moon |
| Exterior cushions | Beige from the factory. Stains from salt and sunscreen are normal wear | All |
| Cleaning | Done by external providers every Saturday. The Lefkas fleet is 45 boats. No in-house cleaning team | Lefkas base |
| Anchor winch | Inspected at the start and end of each charter, documented on video. Service books held for all boats | All |

## How we answer

First reply, same day:

> Thank you for your email. I am sorry about the problems you had. I am reviewing the details with our team and the technician, and I will come back to you by the end of this week.

Point-by-point reply:

> Thank you for sharing your concerns. We reviewed each point with our team. [Item 1: cause and what we did.] [Item 2: cause and what we did.] Most of these points were minor and are now resolved. If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Goodwill offer to a direct guest (confirm the amount with the team first, see [D-036](../decisions/open.md#d-036)):

> We value your feedback. As a gesture of goodwill, we would like to offer you a voucher toward a future charter with us. The voucher is not redeemable for cash.

Broker, goodwill offer:

> As a gesture of goodwill, we would like to offer your guests a voucher toward your next charter, booked through your agency. Please check with us before you offer it to the guests.

During the charter:

> Thank you for your email. We are in contact with our technician. We will see you on Friday to discuss it. Enjoy the rest of your week on board.

Late return request:

> Unfortunately, returning to the base a day later is not possible. I can arrange the return for 17:00 instead of 16:00 if you refuel at our base. Let me check and get back to you.

Spam incident:

> We sent an email by mistake. Please delete it and do not click any links or open any attachments. We have already fixed the issue.

## Escalation

| Situation | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Technical complaint during the charter | Technical team, then discuss at Friday check-out |
| Goodwill amount | Director agrees the amount. Broker vouchers are checked with us first |
| Owner of the yacht affected | Owner's agent joins the internal review |
| Owner or yacht management enquiry | Sales and Fleet Development Manager (sales mailbox), not the complaints flow |
| Winter storage request | Technical mailbox. No rates or answers found, please confirm |

Complaints seen with no full reply in the mailbox (2026-09): anchor winch and bow thruster, RPM display and sail rope, rope safety rails, deck table, missing bucket, a lost ring. An approved reply for technical complaints during a charter: not found – please confirm.
