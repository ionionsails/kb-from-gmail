# Security deposit and damage waiver

Every bareboat charter needs one of two things: Option A, independent deposit insurance with the full deposit held on a card, or Option B, the Damage Waiver bought on arrival with a smaller refundable hold.

Per-boat amounts are also on the boat pages, see [Fleet](../fleet/fleet.md). The general table below is the reference.

The offer can show both a full deposit line and a Damage Waiver line. They are alternatives, not cumulative. The reduced line says: valid ONLY for insured skippers OR mandatory damage waiver.

The client can book without choosing. The choice can be made later. Own insurance must still be arranged before arrival. The client may also decide on arrival, as long as the Damage Waiver is the fallback.

Boats outside our fleet (partner boats) follow the partner's deposit terms. We check with the partner. Partner vouchers or waivers do not apply with us.

## How we answer

Two options for the deposit (direct clients and brokers):

> You can choose between two options. Option A: you arrange independent deposit insurance before arrival, for example through Yachtpool, Hamburg or another insurer. We then pre-authorise the full security deposit on your card at check-in and release it after the charter. Option B: if you arrive without insurance, you take the Damage Waiver through Yachtpool at our office. It has a non-refundable fee that depends on the yacht, and we hold 1,000 EUR on your card in case of damage. You can insure that 1,000 EUR on the spot too. Both options are fine with us.

Booking before choosing:

> You can book now without choosing. You can decide later between independent insurance and the Damage Waiver. If you take independent insurance, it needs to be arranged before arrival. We will send the booking documents in a separate email.

Own insurance, does the full deposit still apply:

> Yes. The insurance needs to cover the entire deposit amount and be valid until the embarkation date. We still pre-authorise the full deposit on a card at check-in and release it after the charter.

Damage Waiver procedure (insert the fee for the boat from the table):

> At check-in you pay a non-refundable fee for this yacht to Yachtpool at our office. We also hold 1,000 EUR on a card as a pre-authorisation in case of damage. Your maximum liability is the fee plus the 1,000 EUR. Blocked toilets and gross negligence are not covered.

With our skipper:

> With our skipper on board the security deposit is reduced by half. You do not need to buy the Damage Waiver or arrange separate insurance. We pre-authorise the reduced amount on your card at check-in. You remain responsible for the interior, the equipment and anything the guests lose or damage. If you are named as main skipper, the full deposit applies.

Broker or third party paying the deposit:

> The deposit is a pre-authorisation on a credit or debit card on the day of embarkation. The cardholder needs to be at the base and sign the slip. We cannot do this remotely or accept a third-party authorisation. At the office we can do both the deposit and the Damage Waiver, not at the broker's office.

Cash:

> We need at least 500 EUR on a card as pre-authorisation. The rest can be cash if the guest has independent insurance covering the deposit. The Damage Waiver fee and its 1,000 EUR hold need a card. We do not accept American Express for the deposit.

Release:

> We release the deposit after the boat is returned and checked. It usually takes 5 to 10 working days to show in your account, depending on your bank. I can send you a release confirmation to give to your bank.
