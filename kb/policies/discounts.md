# Discounts and offer prices

Offers show the list price, then the early booking or special week discount, then our extra discount. There is no official discount table per boat and week yet, so quote only from a current offer.

## How we answer

Early booking offer:

> We are currently offering an early booking discount for 2027. I have also added our extra IonionSails discount. Offer prices are valid at the time of this email, so let me know if you would like an option on the yacht.

Client asks for a lower price:

> The price in the offer already includes our best discounts. Let me check if I can add something for your booking and get back to you.

Returning client:

> Nice to hear from you again. As a returning guest you have an extra 5% discount with your personal code.

Two yachts:

> As you are booking two yachts for the same period, I have added a further discount on the combined price.

Do not promise a free outboard or SUP before the team agrees ([D-014](../decisions/open.md#d-014)).
