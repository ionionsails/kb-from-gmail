# Booking documents and what we need from the client

After a booking we send the documents in a separate email with a payment link. The booking is officially confirmed when the first payment arrives. Later we send a second email asking for the details we need.

The back office handles the charter documentation.

## How we answer

First reply to a booking:

> Thank you for your booking. I will send you the booking documents in a separate email shortly. You will find a payment link in it. After that, I will send you a second email with useful information for your charter and the details we need from you.

Documents email:

> Please find the booking documents attached. Your Booking Confirmation has the terms of payment, our bank details and a breakdown of all costs. The Charter Party is also included. Please sign it and send a copy back to us. The Boarding Pass has the information about your yacht and a link to your crew list.

Details needed:

> Please send us the full address of the charterer for the charter party, and the crew list through the link in your Boarding Pass. If you need a transfer, please fill in the attached form. Let us know your estimated arrival time so we can book your appointment at our office.

Broker, before sending the confirmation:

> Before I send the confirmation, please let me know the name of the charterer and the number of people on board.

Check the crew list deadline ([D-008](../decisions/open.md#d-008)) before you write a number of weeks.
