# Do you want to sail with your pet?

## If you wish to charter a yacht or a catamaran from Lefkada but you have nowhere to leave your dog or your cat, we have the solution for you!

Ionion Sails is the only really pet friendly chartering company in Lefkada.

Enjoy your sailing holidays having your four-legged companion with you thanks to our pet friendly policy:

- In the base of Ionion Sails in Lefkada you will be offered pet keeping services during your check in time and your preparation for your sailing holidays in the Ionian.
- Our customers who bring their pets on board are always entitled to early check in so that our furry friends do not have to wait under the hot summer sun.
- In order to sail in the Ionian with your little friend you will need a valid health passport and the extra cost of biological cleaning that is carried out after the check out.
- **PET BONUS** – If you would like to make use of our **dog raft free of charge**, please let us know during the planning and booking stage.

For more information, please contact us at: support@ionionsails.com
