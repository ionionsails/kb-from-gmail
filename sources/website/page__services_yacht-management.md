# 360° Yacht Management

## Dedicated service. Personal attention. And a team that treats your yacht exactly as they treat their own.

## Enjoy carefree sailing vacations and a genuine return from your own yacht.

Whether you are considering your first yacht investment or you already own a vessel and want it working for you, Ionion Sails offers a complete, in-house management program designed to take care of everything, so you simply enjoy the sea.

From the moment your yacht joins our fleet, our dedicated team handles every detail of its operation: maintenance, crewing, licensing, marketing, bookings and beyond. This is 360° management: professional, personal and fully transparent.

*“No matter how emotional your yacht purchase was, we can turn it into a smart investment.”*

Ionion Sails was founded on a simple idea: manage every yacht as if it were our own. It was in 1995 that **Vangelis and Gabriel** took on the management of their very first vessel. More than fifteen years later, that same commitment drives everything we do.

Today our fleet has grown and our team has expanded, but our standards and principles have not changed. Every yacht in our care receives the same focused attention, the same meticulous maintenance, and the same personal service as the first.

**What sets us apart is simple:** the majority of our fleet is privately owned by the directors and staff of Ionion Sails. We are not just managers, we are owners too. We know exactly what is at stake, and we treat your yacht accordingly.

Unlike most charter and management companies that rely on seasonal subcontractors, the Ionion Sails team is the same, consistent group of professionals throughout the entire year.

Every person who works on your yacht, from our technicians and riggers to our skippers and hostesses, is employed exclusively by us. This means complete accountability, zero middlemen and no inflated costs from external partners.

**Our in-house team handles the full cycle of your yacht’s life with us:**

- Pre-season commissioning and sea trials
- In-season maintenance, technical support and crew management
- End-of-season winterisation and deep servicing
- Year-round insurance coordination and licensing compliance

**This is what A-to-Z management truly means.**

You should always know exactly what is happening with your yacht. That is why Ionion Sails provides every owner with access to our dedicated online platform, giving you real-time visibility into every aspect of your vessel’s management.

**Through your owner portal you can:**

- View your yacht’s booking schedule and availability in real time
- Track your yacht’s location and operational status 24/7
- Access checkout videos, condition reports and the full photo-service log
- Review maintenance records, cost breakdowns and income statements

*No surprises. No unanswered questions. Just clear, honest reporting from a team you can trust.*

Your yacht is a significant asset. We treat it that way. Ionion Sails operates its own private drydock and dedicated mooring facilities, ensuring that maintenance work is carried out under our direct supervision, not outsourced to whoever is cheapest or most available.

Every member of our team works to strict technical specifications that we have refined over more than fifteen years of hands-on yacht management. These are the same standards our owners and management team hold for their own yachts.

**The result?** Consistently excellent feedback from charterers, long-term partnerships with boat owners, and a fleet that stays in outstanding condition season after season.

A well-maintained yacht is only half the equation, **it also needs to be booked**. Our commercial team has been working the international charter market since 2007, attending industry conferences and exhibitions across Europe and beyond, every single year.

Ionion Sails has forged exclusive partnerships with hundreds of travel agencies, sailing clubs and corporate groups worldwide. Your yacht is listed with the most respected brokers globally and promoted through our own unique sailing events, flotillas and an ever-growing base of returning clients.

The outcome is a busy booking calendar and a healthy income from your vessel. **Season after season**.

Many companies offer management and leasing programs as an entry point into yacht ownership, structured contracts that help offset costs in the early years. But when those contracts come to an end, most owners are left to navigate the next chapter on their own.

If your yacht is exiting a financial or leasing program and you want to continue chartering it professionally in Greece, Ionion Sails is ready to take over, **from day one.**

**Our transition service covers the complete handover process from A to Z:**

- Arranging the pre-purchase or post-program survey
- Coordinating the official handover from the previous fleet operator
- Establishing or transferring the professional yacht licence and flag registration
- Delivering the yacht to our base in Greece
- Winterisation, full refit and technical recommissioning
- Launching the yacht into active charter with our commercial network

Pre-owned yachts transitioning from a major program often arrive in excellent structural condition but need refreshing and repositioning. Our experienced team knows exactly what it takes to bring a yacht back to its best, and to present it compellingly to today’s charter market.

*Has your yacht just exited a management program? Get in touch and we will handle everything, from the paperwork to the first booking.*
