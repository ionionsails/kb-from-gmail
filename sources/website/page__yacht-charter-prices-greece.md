# 2026 Yacht Charter Prices in Greece

## Weekly bareboat charter costs for the Ionion Sails fleet.

The table on this page shows both list prices and discounted charter rates for our full fleet of sailing yachts and catamarans for the 2026 sailing season in Greece. All prices are per boat, per week, in euros (€) and apply to bareboat charters.

The Greek sailing season is typically divided into three periods. Early season (Period A) runs from April to June, high season (Period B) covers July and August, and late season (Period C) includes September and October. How much your charter will cost depends mainly on the type of yacht you choose, if it is a newer or older model and the period you sail.

Catamaran list prices range from €6,400 in the early and late season to up to €17,500 during high season for new, top-of-the-range models. Sailing yachts start from around €3,000 in the low season and can reach €11,200 for larger yachts during peak summer weeks.

At Ionion Sails, you do not have to pay list prices. Below is a full, week-by-week breakdown showing each yacht’s weekly rental price throughout the season. Simply click on a week in the table to visit the individual yacht page, where you can explore specifications, onboard amenities, and submit your charter request.
