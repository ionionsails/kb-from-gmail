# Atokos Sailing Guide

## Although it is a private island visitors are allowed to visit the island and swim in the crystal-clear turquoise waters.

Atokos is located 5 miles southwest of Kastos. It is a pure, private island owned by the shipowning family of Panagiotis Tsakos.

Generally speaking the island is uninhabited, you will likely only see some goats wandering around. Atokos is certainly worth visiting and is among the areas of NATURA 2000.

One House Bay

You will be astonished by the water colour and the silence of this spot. If you look closely you will notice a house and a chapel that belongs to Mr. Tsakos (a greek shipowner).
