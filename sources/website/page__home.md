## THE PREMIUM IONIAN CRUISE EXPERIENCE

Our newly formed Ionion Cruise offers fully private day cruises from Lefkas to explore secluded beaches and nearby islands – no groups, no sharing, just your journey, your way. From powerful RIBs to sleek motor boats and luxurious catamarans, each vessel is skippered and selected for smooth, stylish exploration.

If you are an experienced sailor and comfortable handling all sailing duties then a bareboat charter is a good fit. If you want to relax and let a local skipper with knowledge of all the best spots to explore guide you then Ionion Sails has the perfect captain for you and your group!

## IONION GROUP OF COMPANIES

Ionion Sails, Saronic Sails, Ionion Cruise and Spot-a-Yacht operate together under **"the Group of Ionion Sails"** to provide a range of yacht services across Greece. **Ionion Sails** offers yacht charters in the Ionian Sea, **Saronic Sails** covers the Saronic Gulf, and **Spot-a-Yacht** focuses on yacht sales. The group delivers under one unified umbrella tailored expert solutions for chartering, purchasing and selling yachts.
