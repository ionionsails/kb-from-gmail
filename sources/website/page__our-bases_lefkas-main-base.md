# Lefkas Charter Base

## Lefkas connection by bridge to the mainland makes it the ideal spot to start your sailing adventure!

Lefkas is a beautiful island connected to the Greek mainland via a small bridge! The connection of the island with the national roadway network of Greece makes it easily accessible from wherever you are. It is the perfect spot to start your sailing vacations in the Ionian Sea!

You can reach the Ionion Sails main base by car from Athens, by Ferry through the nearby port of Igoumenitsa or by plane through the nearby airports of Corfu, Kefalonia or Preveza (Aktion/ PVK Airport is only 20 mins driving distance from Lefkas).

## Lefkas Mooring Spot

The Ionion Sails mooring spot is located in Lefkas Main Port, next to the city centre and has more amenities than any classic harbour in Greece.

Our berths offer dock refuel services with a truck, mobile workshop right in front the berths, mooring lines, harbour master & crew, free provision and water supply.

All the above make your mooring as also your check in & out a piece of cake!
