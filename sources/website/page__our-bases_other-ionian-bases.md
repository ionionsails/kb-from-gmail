# Other Ionian Bases

## Choose the Ionion Sails base you desire and charter your dream yacht from there!

Even though Lefkada is ideal as a starting point for your sailing holidays, you can always choose and check in or check out in one of the basis of yacht chartering and customer services of Ionion Sails.

## Corfu Charter Base

The base of Ionion Sails in Corfu is located in one of the safest marinas in Greece, the Gouvia Marina.

Being only 6 kilometers away from the city of Corfu and just 7 kilometres away from the airport of the island, access to the Marina is really easy.

In the marina of Corfu Ionion Sails welcomes yachts of extended sailing trips and one way sailing trips from/to our bases either in Athens or in Lefkada and conducts maintenance and repairing tasks.

## Services:

- Crewed Chartering
- One Way Chartering
- Long term Chartering
- Provision
- Maintenance / Repairs
- WC/ Showers
- Mooring Lines
- Transfer
- Laundry/ Cleaning

## Preveza Charter Base

In the city of Preveza, Ionion Sails yachts are accommodated in the most state of the art marina in Greece, the Preveza City Marina.

Being within the heart of the city as well as just 7 kilometres away from the Aktion airport, easy accessibility is granted.

Ionion Sails accommodates in Preveza yachts of extended sailing trips, of one way sailing trips from/to our bases in Athens or in Lefkada and also conducts repairing and maintenance tasks.

## Services:

- One Way Chartering
- Long term Chartering
- Provision
