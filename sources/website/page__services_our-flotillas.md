# Flotillas from Lefkas

## Join a flotilla and join the fun!

## Join a Flotilla with Ease!

Flotillas set sail from Lefkas almost every week during the summer months. They offer the perfect blend of structure and freedom, allowing you to meet new friends and forge unforgettable memories. Dive into the adventure that awaits you on the sparkling Ionian Sea!

Booking a yacht to participate in a flotilla from Lefkas is as straightforward as our standard reservation process. All flotillas from Lefkas are run by our trusted partners Meraki Sailing while Ionion Sails provides our yachts, skippers and additional services to enhance your experience. Simply select your preferred yacht from our diverse fleet and let us handle all the details. Please note, there is a modest additional fee for flotilla participation which covers the organisation and management of the flotilla itself. This fee varies depending on the size of the boat.

In addition to scheduled flotillas throughout the summer our boats and clients may also participate in occasional themed flotillas which bring together participants with common interests to enjoy a week of sailing and activities. Read more about upcoming themed flotillas below.

**For more details on flotillas from Lefkas, contact us today!**

Ionion Sails is proud to collaborate with IonianMode in offering a 10-day Training Flotilla around the Peloponneseduring during spring and autumn. This unique sailing experience is designed for newly qualified skippers, individuals seeking to enhance their sailing skills, and families or groups aiming to learn together.

Participants will have the opportunity to skipper their own yachts, provided by Ionion Sails, with continuous support from IonianMode’s RYA-certified instructors. The flotilla will visit 10 destinations, offering ample sailing opportunities, mooring practice, and swim stops in the picturesque bays of the Ionian Sea. Daily briefings will cover essential topics such as navigation, weather, safety, and mooring techniques.

Our diverse fleet includes various yacht options, from 3-cabin monohulls to 6-cabin catamarans, accommodating different group sizes and preferences. This combination of practical training and holiday adventure is designed to build confidence and competence in a supportive environment.
