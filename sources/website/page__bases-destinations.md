# Sailing from Lefkas

## Lefkas is one of the best places to start discovering the Ionian Islands while sailing!

Easily accessible from the airport in nearby Preveza, Lefkas (Lefkada) is the perfect place to start discovering the Ionian Islands while sailing in the opal waters of the Ionian Sea.

World famous islands such as Kefalonia, Ithaca, and Kalamos are all just a few nautical miles away from Lefkas. Further to the south is the popular island of Zakynthos (Zante) with its famous shipwreck beach. To the north the islands of Paxoi and Antipaxos are among other popular destinations in Greece as are the mainland areas of Parga and Syvota while the world famous island of Corfu offers enough to occupy for days!

Because of the small distance between the islands the whole eastern section of the Ionian Sea is weather protected and sunny most days of the year, but also windy enough to enjoy sailing!
