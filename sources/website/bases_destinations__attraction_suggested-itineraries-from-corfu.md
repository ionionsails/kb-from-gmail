# Sailing Itineraries from Corfu

## A 7 day sailing adventure from Corfu!

## Suggested itineraries for your sailing vacation starting from Corfu.

In Corfu our base is located to Gouvia Marina and is easily accessible with a Ferry from Italy, by car through Igoumenitsa or via air through Corfu Airport.

## Gouvia Marina

At Corfu we share our base with the famous agency Kiriacoulis Med. This is the starting point of your sailing vacations!

## Gouvia to Petritis (15 N.M)

The Best way to start your Sailing trip, since you need only 2 h to get to from the one side of Corfu to the other. Distance: 15 NM

## Petritis

Petritis is a traditional Harbour that is famous for its amazing taverns with fresh fish and the clear waters.

## Petritis to Paxoi (Laka) (18,5 N.M)

From Corfu to Paxous you will have the oportunity to stop and swim in any of the small beautiful bays you will find on your route. Distance: 18,5 NM (2,5h sailing).

## Paxoi - Laka

The worldfamous Paxoi island has two moorings that you must visit. Laka is the first of them. The amazing landscape and the picturesque village is a breathtaking combination.

## Laka to Gaios (10,5 N.M)

This day your route will surround the whole island of Paxoi. This will offer you the amazing oportunity to see the whole island and choose among its hundred bays to swim. Distance: 10,5 NM (1,5h sailing).

## Paxoi Gaios

Gaios is the central of Paxoi. This small port is located in the middle of a canal between Aghios Nikolaos island and Paxoi. This place is famous for its relaxing bars and its traditional taverns.

## Gaios to Antipaxoi (4,5 N.M)

This day you will go from Paxous to Parga but you cannot do that if you dont first stop at the breathtaking island of Antipaxoi. 4.5 NM (0.5h sailing).

## Antipaxoi - Swim stop

One of the natural wonders of Ionian sea! Secluded beaches and incredible crystal clear waters invite you for a dive. Antipaxoi beaches have been voted as one of the most beautiful beaches of the world!

## Antipaxoi to Parga (11,5 N.M)

The second half of your day you will sail towards to Parga. 11 NM (1.5h sailing).

## Parga

Parga is a small town on the Ionian coast of mainland Greece. Although is not an island, Parga is famous because of its amazing scenic beauty.

## Parga to Sivota (11,5 N.M)

Sailing next to mainland of Greece is an amazing experience! You will see the mountains of Epirus touching the opal waters of Ionian sea. 11.5 NM (1.5h sailing).

## Sivota

The Carribean of Med! From the first moment the incomparable beauty enchants the visitor's eye. Sivota is built around a bay in which exist small islands like fjords.

## Sivota to Agni/Corfu (21,5 N.M)

Sailing to the northest side of Greece you will return back to Corfu. 21.5 NM (3h sailing).

## Agni Moore

Agni, in Greek means pure. This place is still virgin, whille its a hidden old fisherman harbour.

## Agni/Corfu to Gouvia Marina

The way of return. You must be at Gouvia Marina before 16:00, so you must start your day early in order to make as many swimming stops as you can at the famous Corfu beaches. Distance: 7 N.M (1 hr sailing)
