# Catamaran Charter Prices in Greece 2026

## Weekly bareboat charter costs for our catamarans.

The table below shows our 2026 catamaran charter prices for Greece, with both list prices and our actual discounted weekly rates. All prices are per boat, per week, in euros (€) and cover bareboat charters. If you are comparing options, catamarans are priced higher than monohull sailing yachts, but offer significantly more living space, a stable platform, and the ability to comfortably accommodate larger groups.

Greece’s sailing season runs from April to October and is split into three periods. Early season (Period A) covers April to June, high season (Period B) is July and August, and late season (Period C) runs through September and October. Catamaran charter prices in Greece range from around €6,400 in the shoulder months to €17,500 during peak summer for our newest, top-of-the-range models. Our catamarans are the preferred choice for groups of six or more, families with children, and anyone who prioritises deck space and comfort over sailing performance.
