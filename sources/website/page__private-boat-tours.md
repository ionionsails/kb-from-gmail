# PRIVATE BOAT TRIPS IN LEFKADA

## EXPLORE LEFKAS WITH OUR PREMIUM CRUISE EXPERIENCES

Our newly formed Ionion Cruise offers fully private day cruises from Lefkas to explore secluded beaches and nearby islands – no groups, no sharing, just your journey, your way. From powerful RIBs to sleek motor boats and luxurious catamarans, each vessel is skippered and selected for smooth, stylish exploration. With premium onboard amenities, flexible departure times, and exclusive experiences throughout, Ionion Cruise is the ultimate way to discover the Ionian – in comfort, in privacy, and at your own pace.

## Suggested Trips

**Antipaxos | Paxos | Gaios | Parga | Mytikas**

*MOTOR YACHT – CATAMARAN – RIB*

Discover the quiet elegance of the Ionian as you journey from Lefkada to the shores of Paxos and beyond. This full-day cruise blends clear-water swim stops with charming harbor visits, sea caves, and off-the-map beaches. Designed for those who prefer unhurried exploration over crowded spots, the route offers a balance of relaxation, light adventure, and scenic coastal beauty all in one day.

**Meganisi | Kalamos | Kastos | Atokos**

*MOTOR YACHT – CATAMARAN – RIB*

This route offers a slower, more secluded take on Ionian cruising. Navigating the quiet islands of Meganisi, Kalamos, Kastos, and Atokos, it’s designed for travelers who want to escape the crowds. Expect calm swim stops, peaceful harbors, and landscapes that feel far removed from the usual routes.

**Mylos Beach | Megali Petra & Avali Beach | Egremni Beach | Porto Katsiki**

*MOTOR YACHT – CATAMARAN – RIB*

A circular cruise around Lefkada’s wild west coast and iconic beaches, this route blends hidden coves, dramatic cliffs, and postcard-famous stops like Porto Katsiki and Egremni. With opportunities to swim, snorkel, and explore sea caves, it’s a scenic and varied itinerary ideal for those wanting to see the island’s most photogenic side from the water.
