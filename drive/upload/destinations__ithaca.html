Ithaca

Ithaca lies between Lefkada and Kefalonia. It is 23 km long with more than 100 km of coastline.

How we answer

Ithaca:

Ithaca is a short sail from Lefkas. Vathi is a large natural harbour with the town on the water, and Kioni is the prettiest village for yachts. There are a few bays with a reef or swell, so your skipper will tell you where to moor.
