Working with brokers

Brokers ask for availability, an offer and an option. We answer with the offer block, place the option, and send the booking documents once the client's payment is in our account.

How we answer

Availability request, type not clear:

Thank you for your interest in our fleet. Are you looking for a bareboat charter, or would your guests prefer a professional skipper and hostess on board? How many people will be on board in total?

Boat free, broker wants to hold it:

The yacht is still available. Shall I place an option?

Option placed:

Thank you for your email. I have placed the option on the yacht, which is valid until [date]. Please find the option confirmation attached.

Boat already has an option:

The yacht already has an option from another party. I have asked them to confirm and I will let you know. Meanwhile I can check the week before or a similar yacht at the same price.

No availability:

We don't have any available options for the dates your guest is interested in.

Booking confirmed by the broker:

Thank you for your confirmation. I will prepare the reservation documents and send them in a separate email.

Same inquiry through several channels:

We cannot offer our boats through multiple channels for the same booking. Please ask the client to choose one agency or to book directly, and I will go ahead with that one.

Skipper licence and crew list are handled in Booking documents.
