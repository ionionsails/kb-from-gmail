Broker payments

The client pays us directly. We confirm only when the money shows in our account, then we pay the broker's commission after we have the commission invoice.

How we answer

Broker books but wants to pay for the client:

Thank you for your booking. For us to accept it, the client must first make the payment directly to us. When the payment is in our account, we will transfer your commission. The yacht remains on option until we receive the payment. The option confirmation is attached.

Proof of payment received:

Thank you for your email and the proof of payment. We will inform you as soon as the funds appear in our account.

Funds received:

We have received your payment, thank you.

Bank slip needed:

I cannot find the payment yet. Could you please send me the bank transfer receipt, so I can identify it.

Payment overdue:

We would like to follow up on this booking, as we have not received the payment yet. Could you please confirm whether the booking is still valid and you intend to proceed, or whether we should cancel it?

Deadline kept:

Thank you for letting us know. We will keep the reservation as it is. I will let you know when the funds arrive.

Early commission:

We can pay your commission early, as long as the net amount for the yacht stays as intended with each payment. We can arrange a payment plan where the first instalment from the guests covers your full commission.

Commission payment question:

I have requested the payment from the ownership company. As soon as I have the bank slip, I will send it to you.
