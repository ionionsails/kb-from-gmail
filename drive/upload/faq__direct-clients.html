FAQ for direct clients

What direct clients ask us most and our approved answer; values live on the linked pages. Most asked first; "Asked" = emails in the pilot. Brokers: FAQ for brokers.

Questions

Is there an early booking discount, and what is the final price?

Let me check availability for your dates and I will send you an offer. The price depends on the dates and on availability, so we cannot give a final price before that. Our offers show the offer price, then the early booking or special week discount, then an extra IonionSails discount (SaronicSails at the Athens base). Two-week charters, several yachts and returning guests can get a further step. Rates change with demand, so an offer is valid only when we send it. The price includes VAT, gas, water, dinghy, linen and towels, and harbour fees for the first and last night. See Prices and Discounts. Asked: 119. Example: offer with discount stack

How does the security deposit work, and what is the Damage Waiver?

The deposit is a card pre-authorisation, not a charge. Option A: your own deposit insurance, arranged before arrival; the yacht's full deposit is then held on a card at check-in. Option B: the Damage Waiver at check-in, a non-refundable fee plus a smaller refundable amount held on a card, which you can insure on the spot. You can book now and choose later. Amounts per boat are on Deposit and insurance. Asked: 90. Example: Option A and B explained

What does a skipper, hostess or cook cost, and what is APA?

The skipper fee is per week, excluding VAT and meals; crewed-only yachts have their own crew fees. You include the skipper in your meals on board or give him a small daily allowance ashore. Crew fees are paid on arrival. Crewed yachts need an APA for fuel, food, supplies and marina fees; the crew keeps receipts and returns the unused balance. See Crew. Asked: 79. Example: skipper fee and APA

We want to book. What happens next, and when is the booking confirmed?

Thank you for your booking. We send the booking documents in a separate email with a payment link. The booking is secured once the first payment is in our account. See Booking documents. Asked: 60. Example: booking acknowledged

Can we charter mid-week or for fewer days? When do we board and return?

Our charters run weekly, Saturday to Saturday. You can use the yacht for fewer days, but the full week is reserved and paid. The yacht returns to base on Friday afternoon, you sleep on board the last night and check out on Saturday morning. Times are on Check-in. Asked: 53. Example: weekly basis

Which extras are obligatory, and what do the optional extras cost?

The end cleaning (Start Pack) is obligatory; the amount depends on boat size. Everything else is optional and paid at the base on arrival. Extras can be added later; we send updated documents. Prices are on Extras. Asked: 51. Example: extras list in offer

Which airport do we fly to, and can you arrange a transfer?

For Lefkada the nearest airport is Preveza (Aktion, PVK); from Athens it is about 3.5 to 4 hours by road. We do not run our own shuttle. We book your transfer with our transfer partner once you send the transfer form. For Nea Peramos see the train question below. Rates are on Transfers. Asked: 48. Example: PVK or Athens

Is the yacht equipment suitable (BBQ, coffee, speakers, sails, water)?

Specs per boat are on the fleet pages. We do not add a BBQ if the yacht has no factory-fitted one, for safety. A Nespresso machine and a portable speaker are optional extras. We do not offer gennakers or Code Zero. Tank water is not certified as drinking water, so bring bottled water. Asked: 41. Example: equipment list

What do you still need from us before the charter?

The crew list (Boarding Pass link or Excel form, not PDF), a copy of the skipper's licence, the charterer's address and phone number, your arrival time, your deposit option and, if needed, the transfer form. Deadlines are on Booking documents. Asked: 38. Example: pre-arrival request

How much do we pay, when, and how?

The payment plan covers the charter fee only; extras are paid at the base. The first half is due within 7 days, the balance before the charter. You pay by bank transfer with the booking confirmation number as reference, or by a secure payment link. The charter fee cannot be paid fully in cash. See Payment. Asked: 35. Example: payment terms

Do you run sailing courses (RYA)?

Yes, from Competent Crew up to Yachtmaster, mainly from our Nea Peramos base, plus private instructor weeks for groups. Coastal Skipper needs night sailing and skipper experience; without it we suggest Day Skipper first. Prices and dates are on Licences. Two-week package for February 2027: not found – please confirm. Asked: 34. Example: Competent Crew

Can you hold the yacht while we decide?

Yes. Send us the yacht and exact dates and we place a free, non-binding option and send the option confirmation. It can be extended on request. See Options. Asked: 32. Example: option confirmation

Something was wrong on our charter. What do you offer?

During the charter, report it straight away so our team can fix it. After the charter we review each point with the team and reply point by point. A goodwill gesture is a voucher for a future charter, not a cash refund. See Complaints. Asked: 32. Example: voucher after complaint

The yacht is at the other base. Can you move it?

Normally no. Guests travel to the yacht's base and we arrange a transfer. In one special case we delivered a yacht Lefkada to Nea Peramos with two of our skippers, fuel and the Corinth Canal fee at cost. See Nea Peramos. Asked: 29. Example: delivery case

How does the skipper work? Can we choose him or get his number?

The skipper is responsible for the yacht; you plan the route together, weather permitting, and you can help sail. We do not share skipper contacts in advance, and there is no one-day skipper. A tip is optional. See Crew. Asked: 29. Example: skipper details

Is the outboard engine included?

The standard dinghy is included. The outboard is a paid extra and not free of charge as standard. Catamarans can take a larger dinghy with a stronger outboard. Prices are on Extras. Asked: 26. Example: outboard not free

Is the boat safe for children?

All yachts have harnesses and tethers, including for children, and jack lines. The skipper briefs everyone on safety at the start. A kids safety net with children's life jackets is an optional extra. See Extras. Asked: 19. Example: child safety

Where exactly is the base? Can we moor our own boat there?

Our private docks are in the Port of Lefkas (no pier numbers); the base brochure shows the location. Visiting boats can moor first come, first served. See Lefkas. Asked: 18. Example: docks and delivery name

Would you take my yacht into your fleet, or store it for winter?

Thank you for your interest. Fleet and management enquiries go to our Fleet Development Manager. Management terms and winter storage rates: not found – please confirm. Asked: 18. Example: owner enquiry

Lost items and small requests (hotels, car rental, Zakynthos)

Found items wait at our office for pick-up. We can suggest hotels and car rental near the base. We do not arrange third-party payments or our own transfers to other islands. Asked: 18. Example: found item

Can we bring or rent a SUP, kayak or other water toys?

You can bring your own SUP or rent one from us; kayaks and a seascooter are also available. We do not have an e-foil. Prices are on Extras. Asked: 17. Example: SUP added

Can we board earlier, or arrive late on Saturday?

You can board earlier if the yacht is ready; guaranteed early boarding is the paid Early Check-in. If you land late, you board Saturday evening and we do the paperwork on Sunday morning. See Check-in. Asked: 16. Example: early check-in

When do we get the deposit back? Can we pay it in cash?

We release the hold after return and checks; your bank then takes some working days, and we send a release letter. At least part of the deposit must be on a card; the rest can be cash only with own deposit insurance. See Deposit and insurance. Asked: 16. Example: release letter

Do we still need the Damage Waiver if we take your skipper?

No. With our professional skipper the deposit is halved and held on a guest's card at check-in. You stay responsible for the interior, equipment and items lost or damaged by guests. If you are named main skipper, the full deposit applies. See Deposit and insurance. Asked: 15. Example: skippered deposit

Is my licence accepted for a bareboat charter?

One person on board needs a valid sailing licence; ICC valid for coastal waters is accepted. A motor-only licence is not valid for a sailing yacht or sailing catamaran. Send a copy of front and back and we check it. If no one qualifies, take a skipper from our team; then no licence is needed. See Licences. Asked: 15. Example: ICC coastal

What if we have to cancel or change dates?

The cancellation terms are on Cancellation: full refund up to a set number of days before embarkation, then a credit only if the yacht is rebooked on the same terms. Moving dates or yacht is normally not possible; airport closures are assessed case by case. Asked: 15. Example: cancellation terms

Can we get a better price or something for free?

The rates are usually already our best. As a returning guest you can use your loyalty code. Sometimes we add one extra, such as a SUP, as a goodwill gesture. If you have a competing offer, send us the details and we compare. See Discounts. Asked: 15. Example: best rate reply

Is the boat well maintained? How does it compare with another model?

All our yachts get winter maintenance at our own shipyard by our in-house technical team, who also support the fleet in season. Each yacht in our offer has a video, real photos and an e-check-in link, and we keep a photo record at check-out. Model details are on the fleet pages; what we highlight and what guests say is on Selling points. Asked: 15. Example: Bali 4.2 vs Lagoon 46

Do you have day cruises?

We work weekly, Saturday to Saturday. From Lefkas we have RIB day cruises, fuel extra; sailing yacht day cruises were fully booked for 2026. See Fleet. Asked: 14. Example: RIB day cruise

Which bases do you have?

Two: Lefkada for the Ionian and Nea Peramos near Athens for the Saronic Gulf (SaronicSails). We have no base in Corfu, Skiathos or Volos; we can check partner boats. See Lefkas and Nea Peramos. Asked: 14. Example: two bases

What is included in the price? Is VAT included?

The yacht price includes VAT, gas, water, dinghy, linen and towels and harbour fees for the first and last night. Fuel, other mooring fees and provisioning are extra. There is no tourist tax on boats. See Extras. Asked: 14. Example: included and VAT

How does the free train to Nea Peramos work?

For charters from Nea Peramos we give free one-way high-speed train tickets between Athens Airport and the base, for all passengers. Send full names and your preferred time slot and we send the tickets. See Transfers. Asked: 13. Example: train tickets

What insurance do we need, and what does the Damage Waiver not cover?

Our only requirement is the security deposit; travel insurance is up to you. The Damage Waiver does not cover blocked toilets or gross negligence. We send the Terms and Conditions on request. See Deposit and insurance. Asked: 13. Example: T&C and liability

Where can we sail? Can we start or finish elsewhere?

We send our Ionian Sailing Guide and suggested itineraries; you agree the route with the skipper on the day. A Corfu start is not offered; disembarking in Kefalonia can be arranged by the skipper on Friday morning. Asked: 13. Example: guide links

Linen, towels and blankets

Linen and towels are included per person. Extra towels, pillows or blankets on request with notice; no beach towels. See Extras. Asked: 11. Example: extra towels

Can groceries be delivered to the boat?

Yes. Order online through our provisioning link or the provisioning list, and our partners deliver to the boat. There is also a supermarket near the marina. See Transfers. Asked: 10. Example: provisioning

Do you have one-way charters between Lefkada and Athens?

Yes, in October from Lefkada to Nea Peramos, on fixed dates. Spring transfers to Lefkada are organised in early April. See Nea Peramos. Asked: 10. Example: October one-way

Is this boat free for our week?

If the boat is taken we say so and send an alternative offer. 2028 is not quoted yet. Asked: 9. Example: alternative offer

Is a partner base's voucher or boat handled the same way?

No. Partner vouchers do not apply to us, and partner boats follow the partner's terms; we check with the partner for you. Asked: 6. Example: partner voucher

Can friends join, or the crew change between weeks?

Yes, no fee for a crew change; the base is easiest. Every guest must be on the crew list. Asked: 5. Example: crew change

What is the difference between bareboat, skippered and crewed?

Bareboat: you sail the yacht yourselves, with one qualified skipper and one more person with some sailing experience. Skippered: we provide a professional skipper, and then no licence is needed. Crewed: skipper and hostess, and a cook on request; The Big Orange Boat and Saga are crewed only, Friends, Horizon and Seabird are skippered only. See Licences, Crew and Fleet. Asked: from the website, not counted. Source: bareboat page

Do you run flotillas?

Yes. From Lefkada flotillas run almost every week in summer, organised by our partner Meraki Sailing. You book a yacht from our fleet as usual and pay a small extra flotilla fee that depends on boat size. In spring and autumn there is also a 10-day training flotilla and an Explorer Flotilla with an RYA instructor from our partner Ionian Mode. Fee, dates and routes: not found – please confirm. For groups that want to sail together without a flotilla see Fleet. Asked: from the website, not counted. Source: flotillas page

Is direct booking cheaper than booking through an agent?

Our website says yes, because the fleet is privately owned and there is no broker in a direct reservation. We have not decided how to say this to clients, so do not promise it in writing. Say that our offer shows the discounts, and that we compare a competing offer if you send it. See Selling points and Discounts. Asked: from the website, not counted. Source: why choose us

Can we bring a pet?

Yes, on charters from Lefkada. You need a valid pet health passport and pay a cleaning fee after check-out; pet owners get early check-in, and a free dog raft on request at booking. Terms are on Options and Check-in. Asked: from the website, not counted. Source: pets article
