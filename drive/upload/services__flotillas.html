How we answer

Flotilla:

Flotillas leave Lefkas almost every week in summer. They are run by our partner Meraki Sailing, and we provide the yachts and skippers. Booking is the same as a normal reservation, with a small extra fee for the flotilla, which depends on the boat size. Send me your dates and I will check what is available.

Do not quote the flotilla fee until the team confirms it.
