Winter base (Nea Peramos Shipyard)

Our yachts are maintained over winter in our own drydock area at the Nea Peramos Shipyard near Athens, from October to April.

How we answer

Winter maintenance:

Our yachts are maintained over winter in our own shipyard near Athens by our in-house technical team. The same team supports the fleet during the season.

Use this for our own boats only, not for partner boats.
