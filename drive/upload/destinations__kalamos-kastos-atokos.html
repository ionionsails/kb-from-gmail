How we answer

Quiet islands:

Kalamos and Kastos are the quiet side of our area. Kalamos is pine covered with small empty beaches, and Kastos has a small village with taverns and safe mooring on the east side. Atokos is private and uninhabited, and you can stop there to swim in good weather.
