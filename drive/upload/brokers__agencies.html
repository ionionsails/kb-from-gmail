How we answer

New agency asks for terms:

Thank you for contacting IonionSails. I can send you our T&Cs and the draft charter party. Our terms are that the client pays us directly and we pay your commission afterwards. You may advertise our boats with a maximum 5% discount from your commission. Payment terms are in the offer.
