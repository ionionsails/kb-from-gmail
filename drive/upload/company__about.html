About IonionSails (company, team, contact)

IonionSails is a family yacht charter company in Lefkada, Greece, part of a group with SaronicSails, Ionion Cruise and Spot-a-Yacht.

How we answer

Who are you:

IonionSails is a family yacht charter company based in Lefkada. We have our own base there, and our sister brand SaronicSails runs the base near Athens. Our team is available by email, phone and WhatsApp.
