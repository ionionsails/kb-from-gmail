How we answer

Skipper fee:

The skipper fee is EUR 1,470 per week, excluding VAT and provisioning. For the skipper's meals you can include him when you eat on board, or give him a small daily allowance so he can arrange his own.

Skipper contact details:

We do not share crew details in advance. Our crew are usually assigned to other weekly charters and last-minute changes can happen. You will meet your skipper on arrival and can discuss the route and arrangements with him then. All our skippers speak English.

Skipper role:

The skipper is responsible for the boat. You choose the route, based on his recommendations and the weather, and you are welcome to help sail. Skippers work Saturday to Saturday, so we cannot offer a skipper for one day only.

Tip:

A tip is entirely at your discretion. Please confirm the usual range with the team before quoting a percentage.

Hostess and cook:

The hostess is EUR 1,330 per week and the cook EUR 1,610, both excluding provisioning. The hostess looks after shopping, breakfast, lunch and the common areas. The cook prepares breakfast, lunch and dinner. Provisioning is paid by the guests.

APA (crewed catamarans):

We ask for an advance of EUR 2,500 by bank transfer, with the remaining balance settled on embarkation. The skipper fee, hostess fee and final cleaning are paid on arrival.
