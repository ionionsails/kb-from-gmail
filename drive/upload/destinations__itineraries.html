How we answer

Suggested route:

A typical week from Lefkas goes Meganisi, Kalamos, Kastos, Ithaca, Fiskardo, Sivota and back to Lefkas. The longest leg is about 17 nautical miles, so the days are short and easy. Your skipper will adjust the route to the weather. I can send you the full itinerary with distances.

Open questions

  • Website vs emails: the Corfu route starts from Gouvia Marina, emails say we have no base in Corfu and do not offer a start there (see Corfu base)
  • The website itinerary pages have errors (Day 12 and 13 text, 8 legs under a 7-day heading). These are website errors to fix
