Complaints and problems during a charter

We acknowledge the same day, review internally, then answer point by point. Goodwill is a voucher, not cash, and there is no standard amount: every case so far was decided on its own.

How we answer

First reply, same day:

Thank you for your email. I am sorry about the problems you had. I am reviewing the details with our team and the technician, and I will come back to you by the end of this week.

Point-by-point reply:

Thank you for sharing your concerns. We reviewed each point with our team. [Item 1: cause and what we did.] [Item 2: cause and what we did.] Most of these points were minor and are now resolved. If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Goodwill offer to a direct guest (confirm the amount with the team first, see D-036):

We value your feedback. As a gesture of goodwill, we would like to offer you a voucher toward a future charter with us. The voucher is not redeemable for cash.

Broker, goodwill offer:

As a gesture of goodwill, we would like to offer your guests a voucher toward your next charter, booked through your agency. Please check with us before you offer it to the guests.

During the charter:

Thank you for your email. We are in contact with our technician. We will see you on Friday to discuss it. Enjoy the rest of your week on board.

Late return request:

Unfortunately, returning to the base a day later is not possible. I can arrange the return for 17:00 instead of 16:00 if you refuel at our base. Let me check and get back to you.

Spam incident:

We sent an email by mistake. Please delete it and do not click any links or open any attachments. We have already fixed the issue.

Open questions

  • D-036 Goodwill compensation after complaints: amounts and forms differ between cases (400 EUR fuel, early check-in refund, 1,000 EUR voucher or free one-way charter, 2 x 500 EUR, 400 EUR voucher, 200 EUR voucher).
  • D-024 Early check-in and return time: the refund of the early check-in fee depends on it.
  • D-037 War and airspace closure: rebooking (not a complaint, but often arrives as one).
