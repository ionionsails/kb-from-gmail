How we answer

Subject: Re: [broker subject]

Dear Partners,

Thank you for your inquiry. Please find our offer below for [Boat(s)], week commencing [Date].

We charter Saturday to Saturday, so the offer is built on that. The price breakdown includes the extras you asked for.

For a crewed yacht add: [Boat] is offered with a skipper and a hostess only. Fees: [skipper fee] and [hostess fee], both excluding VAT and food.

I can place an option for you if you tell me which yacht. The option length is on Options.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Best regards, IonionSails Team

[Yacht blocks with commission and total price] [Standard footer]
