How we answer

Broker version.

Subject: IonionSails: Your Option on our [Boat]!

Dear Partners,

Thank you for your option on our [Boat] for the week commencing on [Date]. The option is valid until [option end date].

You can find the details of the yacht below.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email.

Best regards, IonionSails Team

[Yacht block and standard footer]

Direct client version, one paragraph inside the reply with the offer.

I have placed an option on [Boat] for [Dates] in your name. It is not binding. It gives you priority for the yacht until [option end date], so you do not lose the offer. If you decide before that date, let me know and I will send you the booking documents.
