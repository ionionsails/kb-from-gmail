How we answer

Boarding time:

The latest boarding time is 17:00. You can board earlier if the yacht is ready. We also offer an early check-in service, which lets you board by 14:00 at the latest, for EUR 170.

Return and check-out:

The yacht returns to our base on Friday, no later than 16:00, so we can start the check-out. You stay on board on Friday night, and disembarkation is on Saturday by 09:00. On the first Saturday you are free to leave the port and start your trip, unless the weather is not good.

Late arrival:

Yes, that works. We will welcome you on board on Saturday as soon as you arrive, and we will do all the formalities on Sunday morning, paperwork and technical check-in on board.

Documents before arrival:

As your charter dates are approaching, we need a few details to prepare your booking documents. Please return the Crew List form with at least one valid phone number, send one residential address for the charter contract, send a copy of the skipper's licence, and let us know your estimated arrival time so we can arrange your check-in appointment.

Non-standard dates:

Like most companies in the industry, we work on a weekly basis, Saturday to Saturday. Within the week you can use the yacht for as many days as you wish, for example 4 or 5 days. The weekly price applies.
