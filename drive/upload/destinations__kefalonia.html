Kefalonia

Kefalonia is about 3.5 hours of sailing from Lefkada. Shelter differs a lot between ports, so check the wind before choosing.

How we answer

Kefalonia:

Kefalonia is about 3.5 hours of sailing from Lefkada. Fiskardo in the north is the favourite stop for charter yachts, and Agia Effimia has a port for our yachts with good protection. Poros and Assos are not good for overnight stays when the wind turns. Your skipper will pick the best port for the day.
