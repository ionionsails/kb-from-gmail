How we answer

Learn to sail:

We offer learn to sail holidays with an RYA instructor from our partner Ionian Mode. No experience is needed for the beginner course. Please tell me your level and your dates, and I will check the course options and send you the details.

Open questions

  • Website vs emails: the website places the learn to sail holidays in the Ionian (Lefkada, Meganisi, Kalamos, Kastos, Kefalonia, Ithaca), emails say the RYA courses run from Nea Peramos, with Coastal Skipper in Lefkada in one reply (see D-035)
  • Website vs emails: the website lists a beginner crew course, a course toward Day Skipper, Day Skipper and a fast-track beginner skipper course. Emails list Competent Crew, Day Skipper and Coastal Skipper. The website does not list Coastal Skipper (D-035)
