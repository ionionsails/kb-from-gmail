How we answer

Which bases:

We have two in-house bases. One is in Nea Peramos, near Athens, for sailing the Saronic Islands. The other is in Lefkas, for sailing the Ionian. We do not have a base in Corfu, Skiathos or Volos.

Getting there:

Nea Peramos is about 45 minutes from Athens International Airport. We offer a complimentary high-speed train, and we need the passenger details to issue the tickets.

One-way in October:

During October we offer one-way charters from Lefkada to our Saronic base in Nea Peramos. Let us know if this interests you, and we will send the offer with the available yachts.

Yacht in the other base:

We do not relocate yachts for convenience. We can arrange a transfer for you to the yacht's base. Please check the base on your voucher.
