Payment terms and methods

Standard terms: 50% of the charter fee after booking, 50% before the charter. The booking is secured only when the first payment is in our account. The exact due dates are still open (see below).

How we answer

Payment terms, direct client:

Our payment terms are 50% within 7 days of confirmation and 50% 56 days before the start of the charter. Your reservation is secured when we receive the first payment. You will find the bank details and a payment link in your booking documents.

(Check the open decisions D-002 and D-003 before sending. The wording above is the most recent one in our emails.)

Payment reference:

Please use the booking confirmation number as the payment reference when you make the payment.

Card link expired:

The payment link is valid for 5 days. I will send you a new one today.

Client asks for a different plan:

Our standard plan is two payments of 50%. Let me check if a different plan is possible for your dates and get back to you.
