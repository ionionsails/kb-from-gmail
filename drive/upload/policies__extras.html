How we answer

Outboard, standard request:

All our yachts come with a standard dinghy, about 2.8 metres, included in the price. The outboard engine is an extra at EUR 90 per week. We cannot provide it free of charge at this time.

Larger outboard:

We do not have a 15 HP outboard. We can offer the standard 4-stroke outboard at EUR 90, or on catamarans a 3+ metre dinghy with an outboard up to 7 HP at EUR 280.

Free extras request (boat already at a special rate):

We have already included a SUP free of charge in the offer. As the yacht is already at a very competitive rate, we cannot offer further complimentary extras.

Adding early check-in:

We can add early check-in to this booking. The cost is EUR 170 and the latest boarding time is 14:00. We cannot guarantee it every time, as it depends on the condition of the yacht after the previous charter. I will send you the updated booking confirmation.

Provisioning:

You can pre-order your provisions online through this link, or use the provisioning list attached. Our partners deliver the order to the boat on your arrival. The closest supermarket to our marina is TAOL.

Coffee:

All our yachts have a moka pot. We also offer a Nespresso machine as an extra, EUR 40 per week.

BBQ:

We do not provide a BBQ if the yacht does not have one from the manufacturer, mainly for safety reasons.
