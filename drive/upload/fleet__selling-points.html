How we answer

Why choose you: "All our yachts are maintained over winter in our own shipyard by our in-house technical team, which also supports the fleet during the season. Each yacht in our offer has a video, a page with real photos and an e-check-in link. Our guests often come back, and our skippers and team are what they mention most in their reviews. Tell me your dates and group size and I will check availability."

Do not tell a broker that a direct booking has the lowest price. Brokers sell the same boats on our terms, see Working with brokers. Do not repeat "100% privately owned" or "we own our boats" until D-033 is decided.

Open questions

  • D-033: ownership and maintenance wording
  • Website vs emails: lowest price claim. Website says a reservation without a broker has the lowest charter fee and "100% unbeatable prices". Emails: brokers book the same boats and may give up to 5% from their own commission, and our own rates are described as "usually already our best" (D-046)
  • Website vs emails: ownership. Website says "100% privately owned", mostly by directors and staff. Emails say "privately held" or "IonionSails owns its boats", see D-033 (D-033)
  • Website vs emails: refit. Website says a 6 month dry dock every second year for the whole fleet. Emails say winter maintenance at our shipyard, nothing about a cycle (D-033)
  • Website reviews are mostly from 2023, so the page does not show current feedback. Newer review sources: not found – please confirm
