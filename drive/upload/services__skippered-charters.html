How we answer

Skippered:

You choose the yacht and we provide the skipper. He knows the area well, recommends routes and handles the boat, and you are welcome to help sail. You do not need a licence. If you want, we can add a hostess or a cook. Tell me your dates and group size and I will send an offer.
