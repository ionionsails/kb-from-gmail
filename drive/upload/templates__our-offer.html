Structure of the email text:

  1. Thanks for the interest.
  2. Who writes, and that we help through the booking.
  3. One paragraph on the client's own request (boat size, availability, alternative, skipper or hostess).
  4. Weekly charter, Saturday to Saturday.
  5. Links to the Ionian Sailing Guide and the suggested itineraries.
  6. Offer and Free Cancellation terms are valid for a limited time.
  7. Closing line.
  8. Yacht blocks and footer.

How we answer

Subject: IonionSails: Our Offer!

Dear [Mr./Mrs. first name],

Thank you for contacting IonionSails.

My name is [first name] and I am part of our Chartering Department. I can help you with the booking from the first offer to your arrival in Lefkada.

[One or two sentences on the request. Example: Our [Boat] is available for [Dates]. It has [cabins] cabins, so it suits your group of [number] people.]

We charter weekly, from Saturday to Saturday. Our yachts are privately owned and maintained all year round. The offer is below, with the details of each yacht.

You can find our Ionian Sailing Guide here: [link]. Our suggested itineraries are here: [link].

The offer and our Free Cancellation terms are available for a limited time. If you would like us to hold a yacht for you, tell me and I will place an option.

If you have any questions, just reply to this email. Send me a message on WhatsApp if it's easier for you.

Kind regards, IonionSails Team

[Yacht blocks from the booking system] [Standard footer]

Notes for the sender: add the option sentence only if we place an option. The option length is in Options and the wording is in Your option.
